# Changelog

Semver, staying 0.x until two apps ship on the same version. A 0.x minor may break, and must say so here. The public API is defined in the [README](README.md#public-api). Changing the rhythm model is a major entry, with a note in the specimen's history.

## Unreleased

**Breaking**
- **A list row centers its words in the space it takes (D121).** Each text block in a row was trimmed to its capitals and rounded up to whole cells on its own, so what rounding added always fell under the last baseline: a one-line row beside a face was 12px over its words and 25.58px under them, a dense row on a phone 4.91 over and 14.52 under. A row is now a grid of three tracks (`1fr auto 1fr`): its words, its marks and its end share the middle one and still hang from one line among themselves (D104); a face or an image beside them spans all three at the row's top. The row rounds up to whole cells once (`calc-size()`, or a count from `settle.js` in Safari that holds its height), nothing in it rounds on its own, and what rounding adds is shared above and below: over all 276 row measurements of the specimen (core, Quarry, Almanac; wide and phone; both engines) the two differ by 0.02px at most. The gap between a row's lines is a cell, and half a cell in a dense list, which had halved its padding and not that gap. **What moves:** words in rows, by 0.2 to 7.5px at a 12px cell; at the phone's 9.8125px cell a two-line row whose title's capital is a hair over a cell (core and Almanac) is a cell shorter, and what follows it moves up by that cell. Nothing else moves. **For an app:** `.list-item` is `display: grid` (it was `flex`), so a `gap`, `align-items` or `flex` of your own on a row or its parts no longer does what it did; set `--list-gap` and `--list-align` instead. A grouped row's rule is the row's `::after` now, not a border image on its body. `--settle-n` is registered with no initial value (it was 0).
- **A chat bubble is a shaped ground, with no edge (D122).** Every `.chat-item-bubble` looks different, and no box moves. Others': a faint wash of the text (`--chat-bubble-wash`, 7%, mixed into the surface), no edge, a corner of speech's own (`--chat-shape`, a cell and a half, apart from `--shape-card`), and on the first message of a run the top corner on the speaker's side square (`--chat-head`); a continuing message is round all round, and the bottom corners are round (the tail follows the corner). **Your own (`.cc-own`) is no longer a solid accent**, which read as a primary button: it is a wash of the accent (`--chat-own-wash`, 20%) in the text's ink, still at the row's end. On a bubble the third ink reads as the second, so "(edited)" holds 4.5:1. New product knobs: `--chat-shape`, `--chat-bubble-pad`, `--chat-bubble-wash`, `--chat-own-wash`, `--chat-bubble-bg`. **The D99 knobs keep working, with new defaults:** `--chat-own-bg` and `--chat-bubble-bg` are laid over the wash (transparent; an opaque color replaces it, a translucent one tints it), `--chat-own-ink` is `var(--text)` (was the accent's ink), `--chat-bubble-edge` is `transparent` (was `--border-subtle`). What to check: a layer that set `--chat-own-bg: var(--accent)` without `--chat-own-ink` now has the text on the accent, and `tools/product.js` fails it: add `--chat-own-ink: var(--accent-ink)`, or drop both for the new look. `tools/product.js` holds the words and the second ink on both grounds over every surface, in both themes, on the root and every scope, with the layer's own washes and colors, and refuses a wash that is not a plain percentage or a color it can't read. A context that set `--chat-tail` or `--chat-radius` is as it was; `.cc-dense` takes the smaller of `--chat-shape` and a cell. Flat rows (`.cc-flat`), walls and system lines are unchanged. The Figma file still draws the old bubble (`docs/figma/README.md`). From the bubble options the owner looked at (`experiments/bubbles/`, option C).
- **Scopes: a product nested in a product (D96).** `data-product` may sit on any element, and a product layer may hold `[data-product="part"]` rules after its root rule. Every color role is worked out on `:root` and again on each scope, so a scope that sets a seed recolors everything in it; the five type values that follow the seven are worked out there too (`--type-*`, core's own), so a box that sets the seven must be a scope (the specimen's ratio playground now is). What a layer set carries into its scopes, and what nobody set follows again, so **the knobs that follow another have no default now**: `--seed-neutral`, the kinds, `--seed-topic` and the statuses; `--rp-min`, `--rp-max`, `--body-min`, `--h1-min`, `--label-min`; `--font-heading`; `--density-card-pad-inline` and `-block`. Core reads each as `var(--knob, its derivation)`. Reading one directly (`var(--seed-neutral)`) is empty unless a layer set it; nothing in core or the known apps did. A scope sets its text color, font and `--surface`; with `data-theme` it is a mood, night on a day page or the reverse, and paints its ground. `tests/contract.js` accepts the scope rules and checks contrast for each, its seeds over its product's; `math.js` has `scope()`; `tests/math.js` checks four scopes in both themes against it, and that a scope's ratio moves its text step while the root's chosen step carries in (1,184 checks, was 914). Asked for by the community app, whose communities share one page; the study is `experiments/scopes/`.
- **A surface on a surface is drawn by its ground alone (D88).** A card inside a card, a grouped list, a modal or a float no longer draws an edge, pads one cell where it padded the product's card padding, and steps out only into padding its parent can spare, so a cell always parts the two edges (a card in a one-cell card used to step out onto its parent's edge). Where it opens or closes its parent it steps out at that end too. Its corner is its parent's less the real gap between the edges (18, 6, 3 at the defaults), where it was the parent's less the parent's padding. The light ladder opens: `--bg-raised-2` 0.985 to 0.975 and `--bg-raised-3` 0.97 to 0.95, and `--text-tertiary` goes 0.544 to 0.52 in light so it still reads at 4.5:1 on the third level. To keep an edge on a nested card, set `--card-border`; to keep its padding, set `--card-pad`.

- **Type from seven values (D93).** A product sets seven: H1, body and the label in a wide space (`--h1-max`, `--body-max`, `--label-max`), the ratio in a wide space and in a narrow one (`--rh-max`, `--rh-min`) and the two spaces (`--c-min` and `--c-max`, product knobs now). Five follow, each still settable: the text step is the square root of the ratio (`--rp-min`, `--rp-max`), body in a narrow space is body less a narrow text step (`--body-min`), H1 in a narrow space sits as many steps above body as in a wide one (`--h1-min`), and the label is one size (`--label-min`). `core.css` states them with `sqrt()`, `pow()` and `log()`. **A product layer that set only some of the five** gets the rest from its own seven, where it had core's. `tokens.json` loses `h1-narrow`, `body-narrow`, `label-narrow`, `paragraph-ratio-narrow` and `paragraph-ratio-wide`. `math.js`: `typeFrom()`, and `SEED.seven`, from which `SEED.type` is worked out. A script cannot read a derived knob as a number; read the seven and call `typeFrom()`.
- **Root two is the default wide ratio, and H1 is 72 (D94).** The ratio is 1.26 in a narrow space opening to 1.414 in a wide one (was 1.2 to 1.333), and H1 in a wide space is 72 (was 64). In points, narrow to wide: H1 40.4 to 72 (was 36 to 64), h2 32.1 to 50.9, h3 25.5 to 36, h4 20.2 to 25.5, h5 16 to 18, h6 16 to 18, xl 20.2 to 25.5, lg 18 to 21.4, body 16 to 18, sm 14.3 to 15.1, xs 12.7, the eyebrow 13.5 to 14.3, the label 12 at both ends (was 11 to 12). To keep the old sizes, set `--rh-min: 1.2; --rh-max: 1.333; --h1-max: 4; --h1-min: 2.25; --body-min: 1; --rp-min: 1.125; --rp-max: 1.2; --label-min: .6875`.
- **Color from one seed (D90).** A product chooses `--seed-accent`; the other nine seeds follow from it and each can still be set. `--seed-neutral`, the five kinds and the three statuses now default to derived tokens, `--derived-neutral` to `--derived-alert`: a kind is the accent turned around the hue wheel, a fifth of it apart (event 72°, person 144°, course 216°, group 288°) at 0.85 of its chroma; topic is the accent 0.045 darker; a status keeps its own hue (152°, 70°, 30°) and takes the accent's strength; the neutral is the accent's hue at a chroma of 0.004. Each takes the accent's lightness, kept clear of the ink flip (no lighter than 0.54 where its ink is white, no darker than 0.61 where it is dark), so ink reads at 4.5:1 on every one for any accent inside sRGB, and none takes more chroma than sRGB holds at its hue: a limit that follows the hue in straight segments, written with `clamp()` alone, since `cos()` on a channel inside a relative color differs between engines. **Core's own colors move:** from the default accent, person is `oklch(.54 .141 42.4)` (was `.617 .132 68.7`), group `.54 .092 186.4` (was `.555 .09 212`), event `.54 .141 330.4` (was `.487 .155 313.5`), course `.54 .12 114.4` (was `.519 .127 154.1`), topic `.508 .152 258.4`, ok `.54 .14 152`, warn `.54 .115 70`, alert `.54 .149 30`, and the neutral takes the accent's hue, so the grays turn from warm to cool. **A product layer that set only some seeds** gets the rest from its own accent, where it had core's: set a seed to keep a color. In `tokens.json`, `color.seed` holds the accent alone and a new `color.derive` group holds the rule. `math.js`: `derived()`, `seeds()`, `follow()`, `clear()` and `chromaLimit()`; `SEED.color.seeds` is the accent alone and `SEED.color.derive` the rule.
- **A heading is never smaller than body text (D89).** Each of a heading's two ends is floored at body's, so a heading the ratio would put under body text takes body's size, in the heading's family and weight, with its own line and space after it. At core's defaults h6 goes from 14.5 to 16 in a narrow space and from 15.2 to 18 in a wide one, and nothing else moves; a product with a wide ratio gains more (at 1.333 opening to 1.5, with H1 at 44 to 88 and body at 17 to 20, h5 and h6 were 13.9 and 10.5 narrow and are now 17). There is no knob. H1 is not capped: to keep more headings apart at a wide ratio, raise H1. `math.js`: `typeEnds()` applies the floor and a new `typeSteps()` gives the ends before it.

**Fixed**
- **In forced colors, no edge through a wall post's words.** The system draws every outline in its text color, a transparent one too, so a wall post's, a system line's and a flat row's `.chat-item-bubble`, a box trimmed to its capitals with no padding, drew a line through the middle of its words (Chromium; since D99). They have no bubble and now no edge (`--chat-edge-style: none`); a bubble with a ground keeps its edge there, which is what draws its shape. A removed wall post or system line no longer takes a removed bubble's dashed edge either, which crossed its words in every mode. Three parts that were drawn with a fill or a shadow alone are drawn in system colors there now: a `.chat-divider`'s rules (CanvasText, the unread line's Highlight), the `.chat-typing-dots` (CanvasText) and a `.chat-file`'s edge (a CanvasText outline); outside forced colors nothing moves or repaints. `tests/layout.js`, Chromium in forced colors.
- **A reply's quote no longer shows the top of its third line.** In a narrow column `.chat-quote-text` drew a sliver of the hidden line under the ellipsis, in both engines: its padding given back as margin, and a role's box rounded up to whole cells, let the clip reach it. The clip is measured from the top now, as `.u-clamp`'s is (D105, D122); descenders are kept. `tests/layout.js` reads it from a screenshot, both engines.
- **A checked choice that is invalid shows the alert.** A checked or mixed `.choice` box with `aria-invalid="true"` or `:user-invalid` drew the accent's edge, in both engines: the checked rule was the more specific. Invalid wins now.
- **Invalid in forced colors.** A `.choice` that was `:user-invalid` had no mark there, and a checked invalid one drew Highlight; a plain invalid `.input` (and a box of slots whose control is invalid) drew the system's text color, as a valid one does. Each is the system's Mark now, as an invalid card choice already was (D83). A disabled box of slots stays GrayText.
- **The product check reads a minus sign.** `tools/product.js` read `--night-lift`, `--day-depth`, `--color-lift` and `--ink-flip` with a pattern that has no sign, took a negative one as unset, and passed the layer on core's own colors. Each is read as it is written, on the root and on every scope (the last two were read on the root alone), and one that is no plain number, or is under its floor, is refused by name. What to check: a layer that set one of them to an expression is refused now where it was passed over. `tests/contract.js`; `tests/math.js` checks a night of -.012 and one of -.025 in both engines. From the planning app's draft layer (D117).
- **An invalid field keeps its edge under the pointer.** Hovering an `.input[aria-invalid="true"]` (an input, a select or a textarea) turned its alert edge to the hover gray, in both engines and both themes: the hover rule was the more specific. A box with slots and a choice did not have the fault, and a `.composer` draws no invalid edge. `tests/layout.js`, both engines.
- **One slow run in CI, off by 0.7px.** `align.js` on a throttled phone once found 55 prose blocks off their lines after opening every `details`. It was the browser and not the page: Chromium on Linux keeps one screen density for a whole renderer process, and an SVG image sets it to 1x, so a 3x page's next fonts could be measured hinted, and `1cap` with them. The Chromium phone runs of `align.js` and `layout.js` ask for unhinted fonts on Linux, as a 3x screen has, and `align.js` proves at the end of each that the metrics hold (`tests/chromium.js`, `tests/README.md`).
- **A select's chevron, right to left.** It was drawn at the right in both directions, over a right-to-left select's words; under `dir="rtl"` it is at the left now, where the room for it is.
- **A menu's heading has air under it.** An `.options-card-heading` stood half as far from the first item as from the card's edge, so a lit first item (a list that opens with its first row chosen) ran up under the words. It is followed by half a cell now: its words are about a cell from the item's ground, as they are from the edge. A card grows half a cell per heading. `tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide and touch. From the docs engine's @ list.
- **A control on a full-bleed card can be read (D108).** On a card with a `.media.cc-fill` the words are white, and a button, a ghost button, an icon button, a tinted tag, a choice, a switch, a plain tab and a slider kept the page's ink, nearly black on the dark scrim by day. They read white there, edge, hover and press included, and the focus ring of anything on the card is white, in both themes; the card's own ring, drawn on the page, is as it was. A primary button, a solid or selected tag, a disabled button, a field and a segmented control keep their fill and ink, and so does anything on a card, a float or a modal inside it, or in a scope with a `data-theme`. No new class and no new variable. **What to check:** a product that recolored these by hand (`--btn-ink` on the card's buttons) can drop it.
- **A choice's wrapped label keeps its lines apart.** Past the cell's cap the page's line, two cells, was shorter than a choice's type: a wrapped label's lines ran into each other, and at 340% text in Chromium two of them were counted as one, so the row left the lines and took everything below it along. A choice's line is now the line it is in and never shorter than its type, in whole cells. Nothing moves at default sizes; at 312% a wrapped choice is taller. `tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide and touch.
- **A long tag stays in its member tile.** A tile's cluster was as wide as its longest tag or button, so at 320px and 312% text a small tag ran past the card and the page scrolled sideways (in Quarry; by a pixel in core in WebKit). It is no wider than its tile now, and the tag truncates (rule 11).
- **Two checks in `tests/align.js`.** A textarea that scrolls sideways is reachable: it takes focus natively, unless it is disabled, inert or out of the tab order, which still fail, as does any other scroller with no way in. The band's pixels are read where the screenshot has them: on a phone page that scrolls sideways Chromium's layout viewport is larger than the screenshot, and the check reported a ground "painted above" the band. And sideways scroll is measured against the width the run was given, since Chromium's `innerWidth` grows to whatever overflows on a phone and hid it.
- **Prose in a chat bubble has a width (D112).** A `.prose` is a size container, so in a bubble that shrinks to its content it measured nothing and collapsed to the padding; a bubble that was itself `.prose` and a wall post did the same. In a bubble it is no longer a container: the bubble is as wide as its words, up to the message's measure. With code or an embed in it, and on a wall (`.chat-item.cc-wall`), it takes the measure and stays a container. Code in a bubble steps out no further than the bubble's padding. Flat rows are unchanged. From the membership app.
- **A message keeps its line breaks (D112).** A `.chat-item-bubble` of words and inline marks is `white-space: pre-wrap`, so what Shift+Enter typed shows. A bubble that is prose or holds a block or a `br` is as it was. What to check: a page written by hand with a bubble's plain text on lines of its own between the tags now draws those line breaks; put the text on the tag's line. From the membership app and the planning app, which each set it themselves.
- **A shell with no aside keeps no column for one.** `.shell-grid` reserved `--shell-aside-w` (24 cells) at its end whether or not it held a `.shell-aside`; main now takes the room, in the side shell and in `.cc-top`, when there is no aside or it is `hidden`. What to check: a page that set `--shell-aside-w: 0px` can drop it; a page that kept the blank column on purpose adds an empty `.shell-aside`. From the membership app; three others had set it to zero.
- **A side column's brand is not in the tab bar.** A `.shell-brand` in a side shell's navigation became one more item of the phone's tab bar; it is hidden there now. `.shell.cc-top` keeps its brand at every width. What to check: an app whose brand was its only link home on a phone gives home a `.shell-link`. From the membership app.
- **A production bundle keeps `float.js` and `presets.js`.** `package.json` listed only CSS and `settle.js` as side effects, so a bundler that honors `sideEffects` (esbuild, webpack, Rollup) dropped a bare `import '@iris-cocreative/trellis/float.js'` or `presets.js`, and `Trellis.float`, `Trellis.presets` and `Trellis.transition` never existed. All three runtimes are listed now (`./settle.js`, `./float.js`, `./presets.js`). `tests/contract.js` fails if a script that sets `window.Trellis` is left off the list, and the new `tests/package.js` bundles the three, minified and tree-shaken, from the installed tarball and checks their APIs in Chromium and WebKit.
- **The adoption checks ship in the package.** The documented workflow ran `tools/check.js` and `tests/contract.js <product file>` and read `skill/SKILL.md`, none of which `npm pack` carried, and `tests/contract.js` needs the whole repository (the README, `examples/`, the Figma tools). The product-layer checks a consumer needs (shape, knobs, contrast on every scope) move to `tools/product.js`, which `tests/contract.js` now runs beside its repository checks; the package carries it, `tools/check.js` and `skill/SKILL.md` and `skill/ADOPT.md`, and installs two commands: `npx trellis-check <src> [--budget file]` and `npx trellis-product <product.css>`, also `require('@iris-cocreative/trellis/tools/product.js').validate(css)`. The README's Adoption, `REACT.md`, `SKILL.md` and `ADOPT.md` name these. Still no runtime dependencies. `tests/package.js` (CI, its own step) packs, installs into a clean project and runs every documented command from there.
- **A select's text stays in its box.** WebKit let a `select.input`'s choice overflow the box, so at 312% text on a phone, with a wide face, the page scrolled sideways; it is clipped with an ellipsis now (`overflow: clip`). The specimen's ratio graph widens its right margin to its widest role name, in whole cells. Found by `tests/layout.js` at 312% (D111).
- **The tab bar's current link has its own mark.** Under the tab-bar breakpoint a shell's current `.shell-link` still drew the wide column's accent mark down its start, with its start corners squared. In the tab bar it now takes the selected fill and the mark across its top edge, the bar's edge, its top corners square (D85); `.shell.cc-top`, whose narrow links are a tray, keeps the column's mark. `tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide and touch.
- **A paragraph with two links in it is a sentence again (D106).** In `.prose`, the gallery rule (two or more images on one line) read any two `a` or `img` children as a gallery, so a paragraph with two plain links in it became a three-column grid and its sentence fell into pieces; a record named in a sentence by a chip that is a link did the same. A link now counts only for the image in it, and a paragraph is a gallery only when images are all it holds: images, links with no class and a break. A tag, a chip, a face (`.avatar`), a link with a class or any mark (`em`, `strong`, `code`, a `span`) in it makes it text. A `figure` of images, a paragraph of images and a paragraph of linked images are galleries as before, classes on the images or not. **What changes for content:** a paragraph that mixes images with a mark or a classed link is no longer a gallery; put the images in a `figure`. CSS can't see bare words, so a paragraph of nothing but words and two images is still read as one. `tests/layout.js` checks eleven shapes of paragraph and figure, and that a sentence with a tag, two inline-flex chips and two links keeps every line a line height apart and settles to whole cells, in both engines. From the planning app's owed-cards bench (gap 8).
- **A heading in a row carries no space after it.** In a `.cluster` or a `.row`, a heading's space after (meant for what follows below it) pushed it up off its neighbors' center; it is 0 there now. From the community app's month title between its arrows.
- **`tools/check.js` reads a selector list inside a pseudo-class as one part.** `:where(h1:not(.t-h1, .t-h2))` was split at its inner commas and counted as restyling `.t-h1`; selectors now split only at commas and combinators outside parentheses, and pseudo-classes are removed however deeply they nest. From the community app.

**Added**
- **A product's own colors, by its own names (D117).** A product layer, or a scope, names as many as it has, `--seed-own-gold: #c09642`, in any color. An element takes one as its `--seed`, with `.u-seed` on a part of the app's own or `.cc-seed` on a `.tag` or a `.dot`, and has its roles in both themes: `--seeded` (a fill), `--seeded-ink` (reads as text), `--seeded-tint`, and `--seeded-solid` with `--seeded-solid-ink` (a fill a label stands on, and the label), by a stage's arithmetic. A button takes the last two in its `--btn-*` variables. `tools/product.js` and `tests/contract.js` find each one a layer names and hold its ink and its solid label at 4.5:1, in both themes, on every scope, and for about 6,900 colors across sRGB. `math.js` gives a seed named `own-…` its roles; `ownSolid()` and `inGamut()` are new. Nothing is declared for a product that names none, and nothing core draws moves. From the membership app's axes and alliances and the community app's gold.
- **A night deeper than core's (D117).** `--night-lift` may be under 0, to -.025: past it tertiary ink falls under 4.5:1. The night's grounds must be inside sRGB there. `--day-depth`, `--color-lift` and `--ink-flip` may not be under 0.
- **Phosphor is the default icon set (D118).** `.icon.cc-phosphor` draws Phosphor's raw drawings in strokes, their weight a variable (`--icon-weight`, 8 thin to 16 regular, also a knob), and its outlined files as they are. `--icon-color` is a role on any icon, and gives way to a disabled control and to forced colors; `.icon-tone` and `--icon-tone` are a duotone's second shape; `.cc-mirror` turns an icon in right-to-left text; `img.icon` is fitted whole. `.icon` alone is as it was. `skill/ICONS.md`, in the package: adding an icon with the notice its license asks for, drawing your own with a variant by size, raster icons, and the sizes for Material Symbols and SF Symbols. Trellis still ships and loads no icons. The specimen's icons are Phosphor's. From both apps (ledger 13, 35).
- **A field with something in its box (D116).** `div.input.cc-slots` is an input's box holding a bare `.input-control` with an icon, an `.input-unit` or an icon button before or after it. The whole box is the field: a click on a unit or an icon focuses the control, autofill fills the box, and the box takes the one ring and the states. A button in it keeps its own click, ring and target. A number's spinner and a search's clear stay between the slots. `tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide, touch and at 312%. From the membership app and the planning app.
- **A whole card that is a choice (D116).** `label.choice.card-choice`, a card's title, covers the card, so a press anywhere is the native input's, and the card's own fields, buttons and links stay above it. A chosen card is lit (`--card-choice-edge`, `--card-choice-fill`, `--card-choice-glow`); mixed, invalid (on the input or its radiogroup), disabled and the one focus ring are drawn on the card. A `.card-link` in a choice card is a plain link. `tests/layout.js`, both engines; `tests/contract.js` holds the text on a lit card. From the membership app and the planning app.
- **Button kinds and an outline (D115).** `.button.cc-alert` is destructive and `.button.cc-ok` confirms, filled with the status as a solid tag is, the ink computed from the fill. `.button.cc-outline` takes a kind's fill away: the edge and the label are its color, moved to where it reads as text (no lighter than 0.46 by day, no darker than 0.77 at night), at 4.5:1 on the page and every raised level in both themes, hovered and pressed too. It works on `.cc-alert`, `.cc-ok` and `.cc-primary`; alone it is the text's ink. `--btn-color` is the color it draws in and `--btn-tint` the share of it in its ground. `--btn-bg`, `--btn-ink` and `--btn-border` are set as before. `math.js`: `outlineInk()`. From the membership app; the planning app's tinted destructive button is `.cc-alert.cc-outline` with `--btn-tint`.
- **A quiet tag (D115).** `.tag.cc-quiet`: no kind, the neutral's tint and edge, its words in `--text-secondary`. From both apps.
- **A count on an icon button's corner (D115).** `.badge.cc-corner`, inside the button. Past the cell's cap it grows outward and the button keeps it room in whole cells. From both apps.
- **A translucent navigation bar (D115).** `--shell-nav-bg` and `--shell-nav-blur` on `.shell-nav`; opaque and unblurred unless set.
