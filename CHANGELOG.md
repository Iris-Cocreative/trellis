# Changelog

Semver, staying 0.x until two apps ship on the same version. A 0.x minor may break, and must say so here. The public API is defined in the [README](README.md#public-api). Changing the rhythm model is a major entry, with a note in the specimen's history.

## Unreleased

**Breaking**
- **Scopes: a product nested in a product (D96).** `data-product` may sit on any element, and a product layer may hold `[data-product="part"]` rules after its root rule. Every color role is worked out on `:root` and again on each scope, so a scope that sets a seed recolors everything in it; the five type values that follow the seven are worked out on each text element (`--type-*`, core's own). What a layer set carries into its scopes, and what nobody set follows again, so **the knobs that follow another have no default now**: `--seed-neutral`, the kinds, `--seed-topic` and the statuses; `--rp-min`, `--rp-max`, `--body-min`, `--h1-min`, `--label-min`; `--font-heading`; `--density-card-pad-inline` and `-block`. Core reads each as `var(--knob, its derivation)`. Reading one directly (`var(--seed-neutral)`) is empty unless a layer set it; nothing in core or the known apps did. A scope sets its text color, font and `--surface`; with `data-theme` it is a mood, night on a day page or the reverse, and paints its ground. `tests/contract.js` accepts the scope rules and checks contrast for each, its seeds over its product's; `math.js` has `scope()`; `tests/math.js` checks four scopes in both themes against it, and that a scope's ratio moves its text step while the root's chosen step carries in (1,184 checks, was 914). Asked for by Interbeing, the second app, whose communities share one page; the study is `experiments/scopes/`.
- **A surface on a surface is drawn by its ground alone (D88).** A card inside a card, a grouped list, a modal or a float no longer draws an edge, pads one cell where it padded the product's card padding, and steps out only into padding its parent can spare, so a cell always parts the two edges (a card in a one-cell card used to step out onto its parent's edge). Where it opens or closes its parent it steps out at that end too. Its corner is its parent's less the real gap between the edges (18, 6, 3 at the defaults), where it was the parent's less the parent's padding. The light ladder opens: `--bg-raised-2` 0.985 to 0.975 and `--bg-raised-3` 0.97 to 0.95, and `--text-tertiary` goes 0.544 to 0.52 in light so it still reads at 4.5:1 on the third level. To keep an edge on a nested card, set `--card-border`; to keep its padding, set `--card-pad`.

- **Type from seven values (D93).** A product sets seven: H1, body and the label in a wide space (`--h1-max`, `--body-max`, `--label-max`), the ratio in a wide space and in a narrow one (`--rh-max`, `--rh-min`) and the two spaces (`--c-min` and `--c-max`, product knobs now). Five follow, each still settable: the text step is the square root of the ratio (`--rp-min`, `--rp-max`), body in a narrow space is body less a narrow text step (`--body-min`), H1 in a narrow space sits as many steps above body as in a wide one (`--h1-min`), and the label is one size (`--label-min`). `core.css` states them with `sqrt()`, `pow()` and `log()`. **A product layer that set only some of the five** gets the rest from its own seven, where it had core's. `tokens.json` loses `h1-narrow`, `body-narrow`, `label-narrow`, `paragraph-ratio-narrow` and `paragraph-ratio-wide`. `math.js`: `typeFrom()`, and `SEED.seven`, from which `SEED.type` is worked out. A script cannot read a derived knob as a number; read the seven and call `typeFrom()`.
- **Root two is the default wide ratio, and H1 is 72 (D94).** The ratio is 1.26 in a narrow space opening to 1.414 in a wide one (was 1.2 to 1.333), and H1 in a wide space is 72 (was 64). In points, narrow to wide: H1 40.4 to 72 (was 36 to 64), h2 32.1 to 50.9, h3 25.5 to 36, h4 20.2 to 25.5, h5 16 to 18, h6 16 to 18, xl 20.2 to 25.5, lg 18 to 21.4, body 16 to 18, sm 14.3 to 15.1, xs 12.7, the eyebrow 13.5 to 14.3, the label 12 at both ends (was 11 to 12). To keep the old sizes, set `--rh-min: 1.2; --rh-max: 1.333; --h1-max: 4; --h1-min: 2.25; --body-min: 1; --rp-min: 1.125; --rp-max: 1.2; --label-min: .6875`.
- **Color from one seed (D90).** A product chooses `--seed-accent`; the other nine seeds follow from it and each can still be set. `--seed-neutral`, the five kinds and the three statuses now default to derived tokens, `--derived-neutral` to `--derived-alert`: a kind is the accent turned around the hue wheel, a fifth of it apart (event 72°, person 144°, course 216°, group 288°) at 0.85 of its chroma; topic is the accent 0.045 darker; a status keeps its own hue (152°, 70°, 30°) and takes the accent's strength; the neutral is the accent's hue at a chroma of 0.004. Each takes the accent's lightness, kept clear of the ink flip (no lighter than 0.54 where its ink is white, no darker than 0.61 where it is dark), so ink reads at 4.5:1 on every one for any accent inside sRGB, and none takes more chroma than sRGB holds at its hue: a limit that follows the hue in straight segments, written with `clamp()` alone, since `cos()` on a channel inside a relative color differs between engines. **Core's own colors move:** from the default accent, person is `oklch(.54 .141 42.4)` (was `.617 .132 68.7`), group `.54 .092 186.4` (was `.555 .09 212`), event `.54 .141 330.4` (was `.487 .155 313.5`), course `.54 .12 114.4` (was `.519 .127 154.1`), topic `.508 .152 258.4`, ok `.54 .14 152`, warn `.54 .115 70`, alert `.54 .149 30`, and the neutral takes the accent's hue, so the grays turn from warm to cool. **A product layer that set only some seeds** gets the rest from its own accent, where it had core's: set a seed to keep a color. In `tokens.json`, `color.seed` holds the accent alone and a new `color.derive` group holds the rule. `math.js`: `derived()`, `seeds()`, `follow()`, `clear()` and `chromaLimit()`; `SEED.color.seeds` is the accent alone and `SEED.color.derive` the rule.
- **A heading is never smaller than body text (D89).** Each of a heading's two ends is floored at body's, so a heading the ratio would put under body text takes body's size, in the heading's family and weight, with its own line and space after it. At core's defaults h6 goes from 14.5 to 16 in a narrow space and from 15.2 to 18 in a wide one, and nothing else moves; a product with a wide ratio gains more (at 1.333 opening to 1.5, with H1 at 44 to 88 and body at 17 to 20, h5 and h6 were 13.9 and 10.5 narrow and are now 17). There is no knob. H1 is not capped: to keep more headings apart at a wide ratio, raise H1. `math.js`: `typeEnds()` applies the floor and a new `typeSteps()` gives the ends before it.

**Added**
- **Ground and ink: two neutral seeds (D96).** `--seed-ground` colors every ground and edge, `--seed-ink` every text role; both follow the neutral unless set, so nothing moves. For paper and ink of different hues: linen with forest ink, night teal with bone. In the template, `math.js` (`palette()` reads them), the Figma variables and the contrast check.
- **A switch in a menu row.** A settings menu's row is the control (`button.options-card-item[role=menuitemcheckbox][aria-checked]`), with its label and hint at the start and an empty `span.switch` at its end. An empty switch keeps no room for a label (it stopped 6px short of the row's end), it is lit by its row's `aria-checked` (an app copied the row's state onto a hidden span), it sits at the row's end where `.icon.cc-end` does, it lends no touch target of its own (one would reach into the rows around it), and the row that holds it is not washed as a chosen option (an app turned that off with `--state-on: 0%`). Forced colors light it too. The markup is one empty span and no script. `tests/layout.js` checks each, both engines, wide and touch. From Agent Weaver.
- **Floats: how to open one, and `float.js` to place it.** A `.float` is fixed, stacked over a modal and faded only as a popover, and nothing said so; rule 16 now does: open it as a popover (`popovertarget`, or `popover="manual"` and `showPopover()`), and place it through `--float-x` and `--float-y`, which `.float[popover]` now reads as its left and top (unset, it sits where the browser puts it). `float.js`, optional, beside `presets.js`: `Trellis.float.open(float, anchor, { side, align, gap, margin, onClose })` puts it under (or over, or beside) its anchor at a quarter cell, keeps it half a cell inside the screen, flips it when its side has no room, follows the anchor when anything scrolls or the window resizes, and closes it on Escape (focus back to the anchor), a press outside it and its anchor, or the anchor leaving every scroller's view; it keeps an anchor's `aria-expanded`. Also `toggle`, `close`, `place` and `isOpen`. The specimen's Roles menu uses it, where it had its own forty lines. `tests/layout.js` checks the placing, the flip, the screen's edge, Escape, a press, a toggle, following in a scroller and closing out of view, in both engines, wide and touch. Asked for by Agent Weaver, which has the same code three times. The README notes a trap: a popover is made a block in the top layer, so a tooltip that caps its lines does it on a child.
- **Compact density as knobs.** Five product knobs beside `--density-control-h` and `--density-control-fs`, each defaulting to what was there, so nothing moves: `--density-option-h` and `--density-option-fs` (an options card's rows, 2½ cells in control type), `--density-options-pad` (its padding, a quarter cell), `--density-seg-h` (a segmented control's tabs, 1½ cells) and `--density-seg-pad` (the ring around them, which follows `--density-seg-h` so the control stays three cells). `--option-h`, `--option-fs`, `--seg-pad` and a segmented `--tab-h` read them as their defaults and stay for a one-off. A compact product sets them once in its layer, where Agent Weaver set the same five variables by hand in each context: two cells for a control and a menu row two cells in small type are four lines of the product layer. `tests/layout.js` sets them on the root and measures a segmented control and an options card. (Named `--density-options-pad`, not `--density-float-pad` as asked: it pads an options card, and a tooltip and a plain float keep their own.)
- **A slider, `.range` (D91):** an `input type="range"` on the cells. The row is two cells tall, the track a quarter cell thick, the thumb a cell and a half across; on a coarse pointer the thumb is two cells and the row as tall as the touch target. The track is inked up to the thumb: `settle.js` writes how far along it is as `--range-at`, 0 to 1, when a slider arrives and when it moves, and `Trellis.range(el)` does it for a script that sets `.value`. `.cc-zone` inks the stretch from `--range-a` to `--range-b` instead. Variables: `--range-h`, `--range-track`, `--range-thumb`, `--range-ink`, `--range-rest`, `--range-fill` (a slider that draws a scale paints it) and `--range-thumb-fill`. Hover, press and focus darken its ink; disabled is faint. The specimen's ratio playground and Color study had each built their own; both now use it.
- **A frame keeps a reserve (D87):** `--frame-reserve-start` and `--frame-reserve-end` on `.frame`, or above it. Something that lives in a margin, a column of navigation beside the page, asks for the space it needs; the frame stays centered while both margins cover their reserves, then gives up the other margin, then its own width a module at a time. `--frame-shift` is how far that has moved the frame, and a cover's media reads it to reach both screen edges. `math.js`: `wideFrame()` takes `reserveStart` and `reserveEnd` and returns `margin` and `marginEnd`. With no reserve nothing moves. The specimen's "On this page" column uses it, and no longer sits over the frame in a narrow window.

**Changed**
- **An icon button is `button icon-button`, and the docs say so.** `.icon-button` is a modifier: alone it sets a width from a variable that isn't there and draws nothing. The README's primitives and Public API and the skill listed it as if it stood alone; they now write `.button.icon-button` and give the markup once, `<button class="button icon-button cc-ghost cc-sm" aria-label="…">`. The specimen's own top-bar menu buttons were bare `shell-menu icon-button` and are now buttons. An app wrote it alone 24 times and drew each box itself.
- **`tools/check.js` counts two more things.** `bare-modifiers`: a class string, in markup or a script, that holds `icon-button` with no `button`, or a `cc-*` modifier with nothing core beside it (core's contexts, `.cc-dense` and the scrollbar modifiers, may stand alone). `restyled-own`: a declaration on a class of the app's own that sits on a primitive's element (it learns which from the class strings in the app's pages and scripts) and sets a property the primitive draws; the primitive's variables, its place in its parent (margin, `flex`, `order`, `align-self`, `grid-area`, `position` and insets, `z-index`) and `display: none` are the app's. A class string with an interpolation is not held to the modifier rule. The skill says what a class of your own on a primitive may carry. **A budget written before this does not fail on the new counts:** a kind the budget file has never recorded is reported, and `--write` starts holding it. On Agent Weaver's app it finds 17 bare icon buttons and 52 restylings through its own classes, where it counted 21 restylings in all.
- **Space is counted in cells, not doubled (D86):** the framework no longer says the cell "halves and doubles", and the space tokens are named counts, not octaves. Any whole count of cells is on the grid; halves and quarters live inside things; a product may favor a set of counts that suits its ratio (the Fibonacci counts beside a golden type ratio, or doubling counts), and Trellis enforces no set. - **A mark is a straight line (D85):** a current `.shell-link` or `.outline-link` squares the two corners its accent edge touches, so the mark no longer curls into a rounded corner. In a top bar the mark is under the link and the bottom corners are square.

**Fixed**
- **The runtime scales.** In WebKit at 5,000 blocks, up to 87 ended up off their lines, intermittently, and one changed row took 380ms of blocking work; now 0 and about 50ms. Text in Safari was settled by padding a measured gap, and WebKit's trimmed heights jitter by 1/64px with position, so corrections never converged. It now holds a count of cells as its least height. **The rhythm model is unchanged:** this is how Safari reaches it. The numbers are in `tests/README.md`.
- `--settle` inherited, so a block's space also padded the text inside it. It is now registered and doesn't.
- The reduced-motion override sat outside every layer, so nothing could override it.
- `settle.js` re-settles each time fonts finish loading, not just the first: a font arriving later could make text wrap to fewer lines in a block held at whole cells, which can't notice by itself. `settled()` no longer resolves before the runtime has started.

**Rules**
- Rule 5 says hang is clipped by any ancestor with hidden overflow.
- Rule 6 says half-cell gaps are for clusters; why a cluster's overflow is safe; and that a row with half-cell padding is one thing, its edges on lines and its contents on their own rhythm.
