# C4 Contrast

## What it is

The palette is derived, so a product's seeds decide whether its text can be read. The contract checks that they can: every floor below, for core and for every product layer, in both themes. A seed that breaks a floor fails the contract.

| Ink | On | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| `--text` | `--bg`, `--bg-raised`, `--bg-sunken` | 4.5:1 |
| `--text-secondary` | `--bg`, `--bg-raised`, `--bg-sunken` | 4.5:1 |
| `--text-tertiary` | `--bg`, `--bg-raised` | 4.5:1 |
| `--accent-ink` | `--accent` | 4.5:1 |
| `--text`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-tertiary` | `--bg-raised-2`, `--bg-raised-3` | 4.5:1 |
| The ink computed from each fill | `--kind-person` … `--kind-topic`, `--ok`, `--warn`, `--alert` (a solid tag, a badge) | 4.5:1 |
| `--accent` | `--bg`, `--bg-raised` | 3:1 |

The floors are WCAG 2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for the color of a control against what is around it. `--text-faint` and the borders are decoration and have no floor.

## Why

Before the palette was relational, the accent's ink was white in both themes and measured about 2.5:1 on the dark accent. Nothing caught it, because nothing measured it. A floor in the contract turns legibility into a check like any other: it holds for core, and it holds for a product's seeds before that product ships.

## How it is computed, in plain words

`tests/contract.js` reads each product layer's `--seed-*`, `--color-lift` and `--ink-flip`, derives that layer's palette with `math.js` in each theme, and measures each pair with WCAG 2 relative luminance. It is the same arithmetic `core.css` performs with relative colors, so the numbers are the browser's. The specimen's Color section measures text, secondary, tertiary and the accent's ink in the browser too, from the colors it actually paints. At the default seeds, light: 16.3, 8.6, 4.6 and 4.9:1; dark: 17.2, 11.0, 6.0 and 7.7:1.

## What changed to hold them

A solid tag's label and a badge's count were fixed white, and four pairs missed the text floor: white on `--kind-person` (3.80:1), `--kind-group` and `--warn` (4.44:1) and `--ok` (4.16:1) in light, and a badge's white count on the lifted `--alert` in dark (2.57:1). The accent had the same failure before its ink was computed (D54).

Both now compute their ink from their fill as `--accent-ink` does (D83). That puts the person kind's label in a dark ink (5.13:1). Three default seeds were mid-tones where neither ink reached 4.5:1, so each is a step darker: `--seed-group` 0.563 to 0.555, `--seed-ok` 0.571 to 0.547, `--seed-warn` 0.578 to 0.572 (their white labels 4.59, 4.58 and 4.55:1). Every dark-theme ink is above 8:1. A product whose seed sits in that middle band fails the contract and says which.

## Where it lives in the code

- `tests/contract.js`: `FLOORS`, and the loop over core and `examples/`.
- `math.js`: `palette()`, `ink()`, `contrast()`, `luminance()`.
- `core.css`: the solid tag's and the badge's computed ink, beside `.tag.cc-solid`.
- `specimen.js`: the Color section's measured contrasts.

## How it is checked

By the contract itself, on every push, in CI. An outside product layer runs through the same check from where it lives ([`tests/README.md`](../../tests/README.md)).
