# C5 Forced colors

## What it is

In a forced colors mode (Windows contrast themes, and any browser emulating them) the system replaces the page's colors with a few of its own: a ground (`Canvas`), ink (`CanvasText`), links (`LinkText`), and highlight pairs. Backgrounds become the ground, text becomes the ink, borders and outlines take the ink, and **every `box-shadow` is removed**. Images are left alone.

Core has no `forced-colors` rule. What survives is what core happens to draw with borders, outlines and text; what is lost is what it draws with a shadow or a background alone.

## What survives

Checked in Chromium with forced colors emulated, on the specimen:

- **A surface's edge.** A card's edge plane is a real `border` on its `::after` frame, so every card keeps its outline.
- **The focus ring.** It is an `outline`, so it stays, in the system's color.
- **Controls with a real border.** Buttons (the primary included), inputs, and tags keep their edges; a solid tag and a primary button lose their fill and read as outlined.
- **Text and icons.** All ink takes the system's text or link color. Icons are strokes in `currentColor`, so they follow.
- **Kind on an avatar.** Its shape (circle or rounded square) survives; its ring, a shadow, does not.

## What is lost

- **Current and selected marks drawn as a shadow or a fill.** The current `.shell-link`'s accent edge and a selected `.list-item`'s edge are inset shadows; a selected `.tab`'s line is an `::after` with a background; a segmented tab's selected fill is a background. All of them disappear, so the current page and the selected tab look like the others.
- **The switch.** Its track and its thumb are backgrounds. Both become the ground, so the switch draws nothing, on or off.
- **Dots and badges.** A `.dot` is only a background and vanishes; a `.badge` keeps its number and loses its pill.
- **The state layer.** Hover, press and selected are mixes into a fill, so they are replaced; selected relies on its own mark, which is in the first item above.
- **Raised floats' shadow** (`--lift`). Floats and modals keep their inset outline ([D75](../decisions/D75.md)), so their edge survives.

## Why

A reader who chooses a contrast theme has told the page that its colors are in the way. Respecting that means letting the system's colors win, which core does by default, and making sure nothing that carries meaning was drawn only with what the system removes. Core meets the first half and not the second.

## How it is computed, in plain words

Nothing is computed. The fix that is planned, and not built, is small and known: one `@media (forced-colors: active)` block that gives each mark above a border or an outline in a system color (`Highlight` for current and selected, `CanvasText` for a switch's track and thumb and a dot), and a check that runs the specimen with forced colors on.

## Where it lives in the code

`core.css`: the comment "High-contrast modes drop shadows but keep outlines" just after `@layer trellis.utilities`, which is true and is the whole of the current support.

## How it is checked

Not yet. `tests/align.js` and `tests/pixel.js` do not run with forced colors. This page is questioned until the marks above survive and a test says so.
