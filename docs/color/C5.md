# C5 Forced colors

## What it is

In a forced colors mode (Windows contrast themes, and any browser emulating them) the system replaces the page's colors with a few of its own: a ground (`Canvas`), ink (`CanvasText`), links (`LinkText`), and highlight pairs. Backgrounds become the ground, text becomes the ink, borders and outlines take the ink, and **every `box-shadow` is removed**. Images are left alone.

What core draws with borders, outlines and text survives on its own. What it draws with a shadow or a background alone would be lost, so one `@media (forced-colors: active)` block draws it again in system colors (D83).

## What survives

Checked in Chromium with forced colors emulated, on the specimen:

- **A surface's edge.** A card's edge plane is a real `border` on its `::after` frame, so every card keeps its outline.
- **The focus ring.** It is an `outline`, so it stays, in the system's color.
- **Controls with a real border.** Buttons (the primary included), inputs, and tags keep their edges; a solid tag and a primary button lose their fill and read as outlined.
- **Text and icons.** All ink takes the system's text or link color. Icons are strokes in `currentColor`, so they follow.
- **Kind on an avatar.** Its shape (circle or rounded square) survives; its ring, a shadow, is drawn again below.

## What is drawn again

Without the block these were lost, measured in Chromium; with it, each is drawn in a system color:

- **Current and selected marks.** The current `.shell-link` and `.outline-link`, a selected `.list-item`, `.tab` or `.options-card-item`: a two-line outline in `Highlight`, inside the control. A selected tab's line keeps its own color, `Highlight`.
- **The switch.** Its track is outlined in `CanvasText`; off, the thumb is `CanvasText` on `Canvas`; on, the track fills with `Highlight` and the thumb is `HighlightText`; disabled, both are `GrayText`.
- **Dots** are `CanvasText`; a **badge** keeps an outline as its pill; an **avatar's** ring is an outline in `CanvasText`, at its gap.
- **The state layer** (hover, press) stays replaced by the system's own: selected has its own mark above.
- **Raised floats' shadow** (`--lift`) stays removed; floats and modals keep their inset outline ([D75](../decisions/D75.md)).

## Why

A reader who chooses a contrast theme has told the page that its colors are in the way. Respecting that means letting the system's colors win, which core does by default, and making sure nothing that carries meaning was drawn only with what the system removes. Core does both: the system's colors win everywhere, and the block draws each mark that would otherwise vanish.

## How it is computed, in plain words

Nothing is computed: the system's colors win, and core only chooses which of them draws each mark. `forced-color-adjust: none` is set only on the parts that are drawn with a background (a tab's line, the switch, a dot), so the system cannot paint them over with the ground.

## Where it lives in the code

`core.css`, the forced colors block just after `@layer trellis.utilities`.

## How it is checked

`tests/layout.js` loads the specimen with forced colors emulated, in both engines, wide and touch: every mark above draws an outline or a color that is not the ground, and a switch on differs from a switch off.
