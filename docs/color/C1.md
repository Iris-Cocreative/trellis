# C1 Seeds and roles

## What it is

Two layers of color. **Seeds** are the knobs: ten colors a product chooses, each written in its light-theme form. **Roles** are the tokens a component paints with: a page, a raised surface, ink at three strengths, edges, the accent, a color per kind and per status. A product sets seeds and never a role. A component reads roles and never a seed.

| Seed | Gives |
|---|---|
| `--seed-neutral` | Its hue and chroma to every neutral role: `--bg`, `--bg-raised`, `--bg-raised-2`, `--bg-raised-3` (surfaces on surfaces, R21), `--bg-sunken`, `--bg-hover`, `--text`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-tertiary`, `--text-faint`, `--border`, `--border-subtle`, `--border-strong`. Its own lightness is not used. |
| `--seed-accent` | `--accent` (the primary action), `--accent-ink` (ink on it), `--focus` (the ring), `--gradient-accent`. |
| `--seed-person`, `--seed-group`, `--seed-event`, `--seed-course`, `--seed-topic` | `--kind-*`, one per kind ([C3](C3.md)). |
| `--seed-ok`, `--seed-warn`, `--seed-alert` | `--ok`, `--warn`, `--alert`: status, not kind. |

Four more knobs turn the derivation itself: `--color-lift` (how much lighter a hue is in the dark theme, 0.17), `--ink-flip` (the lightness where ink on a fill turns from white to dark, 0.58), `--gradient-space` (OKLCH) and `--gradient-turn` (40 degrees).

Everything else is a **mix**: a role mixed with `color-mix(in oklab, …)`. A tag's fill and edge are its kind color mixed into transparent; an avatar's wash is its ring color; the state layer is a control's own ink mixed into its own fill ([README, States](../../README.md#primitives)); the scrollbar and the skeleton are the ink; a scrim is black. **A color is a role, or a mix of a role.**

## Why

Before the palette was relational, a product set fourteen neutral roles, five kinds and three statuses by hand, as fixed pairs. Nothing tied one to another, so a product could break legibility without noticing: ink on the accent was white in both themes, about 2.5:1 on the dark theme's lighter blue. A seed holds the relation instead of the value, as the cell does for space and the ratio does for type ([S8](../seed/S8.md)). Change the neutral seed's hue and every surface, ink and edge moves with it, at the same lightness steps, so their contrast holds.

## How it is computed, in plain words

- A **neutral role** is the neutral seed's hue and chroma at a fixed lightness for each theme. The lightness ladder is in `tokens.json` (`color.neutral`): the page is 0.973 in light and 0.176 in dark, a raised surface 1 and 0.213, ink 0.213 and 0.967, and so on.
- A **hue** (the accent, a kind, a status) is its seed as written in the light theme, and in the dark theme the same color with its lightness raised by the lift ([C2](C2.md)).
- **Ink on a fill** is computed from the fill: white when the fill is darker than the flip, and above it a dark ink of the fill's own hue (lightness 0.16, three tenths of its chroma). `--accent-ink` is the one role computed this way today.
- A **gradient** is an interpolation space and its stops, with no direction: `--gradient-accent` runs from the accent to the accent turned by `--gradient-turn`; `--gradient-surface` runs from raised to sunken. Add the direction or shape where it is used: `linear-gradient(to right var(--gradient-accent))`.

## Where it lives in the code

- `tokens.json`, group `color`: the seeds, the lift, the flip, the neutral ladder and the gradient settings. `tools/build-tokens.js` writes them into `core.css` between `tokens:start` and `tokens:end`.
- `core.css`, `@layer trellis.theme`: every role as `light-dark()` of two relative colors (`oklch(from var(--seed-…) …)`). Below it, for a browser without relative colors, the same roles as fixed hex pairs resolved by `math.js`.
- `math.js`: `palette(theme, seed)` derives every role in arithmetic, `ink(fill)` the ink on a fill, `contrast(a, b)` the WCAG 2 ratio. A native screen reads these instead of CSS.
- `product.template.css` lists the seeds and the four derivation knobs a product may set.

## How it is checked

`tests/contract.js` fails a stale `core.css` (one whose token block no longer matches `tokens.json`), a role a product layer sets, and any floor in [C4](C4.md) that a layer's seeds break. The specimen's Color section shows every seed, every role as a light and dark pair, and every mix with its expression.
