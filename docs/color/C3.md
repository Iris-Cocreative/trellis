# C3 Kinds

## What it is

A kind is what sort of thing something is: a **person**, a **group**, an **event**, a **course** or a **topic**. Each kind has a seed and a role (`--kind-person` … `--kind-topic`), and a thing of that kind carries its color wherever it appears: a tag, an avatar's ring and wash, a dot. **Shape and color both follow kind.** On an avatar the shape says it before the color does: a circle is a person, a rounded square (its corner a quarter of its size) is a group.

Status is not a kind. `--ok`, `--warn` and `--alert` say how something stands, not what it is, and they paint the same marks: a status tag, a dot, a badge, an invalid field's hint.

Topic is also the default: a `.tag` with no kind is a topic tag.

## Why

People read a page by what is on it before they read the words. When a person always arrives in the same color and the same circle, and a group always in its own color and a rounded square, a list of mixed things sorts itself at a glance. Shape carries the meaning as well as color, so the kind survives for a reader who cannot tell the colors apart, and in forced colors, where the system replaces them ([C5](C5.md)).

## How it is computed, in plain words

A kind's role is its seed in the light theme and the seed lifted in the dark, like every hue ([C2](C2.md)). A component never paints the kind color directly as a ground. It mixes it: a tag's fill is 14% of its kind color, its edge 42%, its label the page's ink; an outline tag drops the fill; a solid tag (`.cc-solid`) is the kind color at full strength, its label in ink computed from that color: white on a dark one, a dark ink of its hue on a light one. A kind is chosen by a modifier that sets one variable: `.tag.cc-person` sets `--tag-color: var(--kind-person)`, `.avatar.cc-group` sets the ring color and the corner.

## Where it lives in the code

- `tokens.json`, `color.seed`: `person`, `group`, `event`, `course`, `topic`, `ok`, `warn`, `alert`.
- `core.css`: the roles in `@layer trellis.theme`; `.tag` and its kind modifiers, `.avatar.cc-group`, `.badge`, `.dot` in `@layer trellis.primitives`.
- `math.js`: `palette()` returns each `kind-*` and status role.

## How it is checked

The contract checks the ink a solid tag or a badge computes from every kind and status color, at 4.5:1 in both themes, for core and every product layer ([C4](C4.md)).
