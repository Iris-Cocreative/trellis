# S2 The ratio

## What it is

The measure of type. There are two anchors, H1 and body, and a third for the label. Each has a size in a narrow space and a size in a wide one.

Headings step down from H1 by one ratio. Paragraph roles step out from body by another. Both ratios open with the space: the neutral default is 1.2 in a narrow space rising to 1.333 in a wide one for headings, and 1.125 rising to 1.2 for paragraphs. The roles are `.t-h1` to `.t-h6`, `.t-xl`, `.t-lg`, `.t-body`, `.t-sm`, `.t-xs`, `.t-label`, and `.t-eyebrow`, the label one paragraph step up.

## Why

A ratio turns a list of font sizes into one relationship. A product changes two anchors and two ratios, and every role follows.

A ratio that opens with the space keeps hierarchy honest. In a narrow space, headings stay close in size so nothing shouts. In a wide one, the steps open up so the hierarchy reads at a distance.

## How it is computed, in plain words

**A role's two ends.** A role n steps from its anchor has a narrow end of the anchor's narrow size times the narrow ratio to the power n, and a wide end of the anchor's wide size times the wide ratio to the power n. Headings step down, so H3 is two steps below H1: its ends are H1 ÷ ratio². Paragraph roles step out from body: xl is two steps up, xs two steps down.

**A role's size.** A straight line between its two ends, held at each: size = clamp(narrow end, narrow end + (wide end − narrow end) × (space − 320) ÷ 1120, wide end). The space is the container the text sits in, not the window, measured in the reader's text units: `math.js` divides the container's width by the text scale, then multiplies the size by it.

**Line height.** A multiple of the size. H1 is 1.05, and each smaller heading opens by 0.05. Body is 1.45, and each larger paragraph role closes by 0.03. The label is 1.2. Headlines (h1 to h3) round their line height to whole cells, so they stand on the cells. Other text keeps its own line height and hangs (see Type on the cell in the README).

**A heading's own space.** After a heading: size × (0.33 + 0.11 for each step below H1), rounded up to whole cells. In a stack it is the larger of that space and the stack's gap, never the sum.

**The neutral defaults,** in points: narrow space 320, wide space 1440; H1 36 to 64; body 16 to 18; label 11 to 12.

**A known wrinkle.** In every product so far, xs comes out larger in a narrow space than a wide one, because the paragraph ratio opens faster than body grows. CSS holds xs at its narrow size. Accepting a fixed xs, or retuning the paragraph ratios so it scales, is open question 1 in the README.

## Where it lives in the code

- `tokens.json`, the `type` group: `space-narrow`, `space-wide`, the three anchors' two ends, and the two ratios' two ends.
- `core.css`: each role sets `--t-min` and `--t-max` from the anchors and the ratios, and `--t-fs` is a clamp between them along `100cqi`, the container's width. `--lh-h1`, `--lh-h-step`, `--lh-body`, `--lh-p-step` and `--lh-label` set line heights. `--mb-h1` and `--mb-h-step` set a heading's own space. Products set all of these on `:root` (R14).
- `math.js`: `typeEnds()`, `typeSize()`, `lineRatio()`, `lineHeight()` and `spaceAfter()`.
- Tests: `tests/math.js` checks every role's size from the live knobs across eight screens and all three layers. The specimen's math view shows the working under each role.
