# S7 The masters

## What it is

Five references the seed draws on, each for one idea. They are named in the README (Organic interfaces) and in the specimen.

## Why

A named source gives a reviewer a test. If a choice claims to follow an idea, the idea can be read and the choice checked against it. It also keeps the system from collecting ideas without saying where they came from.

## How it is computed, in plain words

Nothing is computed from the masters directly. Each idea shows up as a number or a rule:

| Master | The idea | Where it shows in Trellis |
|---|---|---|
| Kandinsky | Every mark is a point, a line or a plane. | The three measures ([S6](S6.md)); lines are planes ([S5](S5.md)). |
| Mondrian | A few proportions in asymmetric balance, with the empty field doing work. Columns favor 1:2 over equal halves; space is a component, not leftovers. | `.split` is two columns at 2:1 by default (`--split-a`, `--split-b`); space between blocks is whole cells (R6). |
| Frank Lloyd Wright | The unit system: plans drawn on one module, the whole grown from the part. Organic means part and whole share one principle. | The cell is that unit ([S1](S1.md)); the frame is a field of whole 4-cell modules ([S3](S3.md)). |
| Buckminster Fuller | Triangulation and growth by frequency. Detail is added by doubling, never by new numbers. | The octaves halve and double; the isometric lattice slopes 2:1 for maps and constellations. |
| Christopher Alexander | Levels of scale, strong centers, boundaries. Neighboring sizes differ by about two, every element has a center, and a boundary is proportional to what it bounds. | Sizes step by doubling; the cell count is even so the screen's center is a line; an avatar's gap and ring are concentric at every size. |

## Where it lives in the code

- `README.md`, Organic interfaces, names the five.
- The specimen (`index.html`) gives each one a card with the idea in a sentence.
- The ideas themselves live where the table says: the octaves in `tokens.json`, `.split` and the frame in `core.css`, the lattice tokens `--iso-dx` and `--iso-dy`, and the avatar primitive.

The changelog does not record when the masters were first named, so `since` is unrecorded.
