# A5 · Element

## What it is

The primitives: the smallest named things, each one archetype whose variants only change its own variables.

## Its parts

- **Type roles:** `.t-h1` to `.t-h6`, `.t-xl`, `.t-lg`, `.t-body`, `.t-sm`, `.t-xs`, `.t-label`, `.t-eyebrow`, plus `.t-serif` and `.t-mono`. `.snap` and `.snap-x` put other text on a line; `.settle` rounds any block to whole cells; `.cap-center` centers a label's capitals on a control beside it.
- **`.tag`**: the archetype. A height, a corner, padding equal to the corner, a hairline edge and a tint mixed from one kind color, a label trimmed to its capitals. Parts `.tag-label`, `.tag-dot`, `.tag-remove`.
- **`.button`, `.icon-button`**: heights 1½ to 4 cells, padding half the height, label trimmed to cap height. Part `.button-label`.
- **`.avatar`**: the photo is the box, on whole cells; gap and ring are spread shadows outside it. Shape is kind: circle for a person, rounded square for a group. Parts `.avatar-initials`, `.avatar-dot`, `.avatar-stack`, `.avatar-more`.
- **`.field`, `.input`**: one box for input, select and textarea. Parts `.field-label`, `.field-hint`, which start at `--field-inset`, where the input's text starts.
- **`.tabs`, `.tab`**: whole cells tall; `.cc-seg` is the same tabs as a segmented control.
- **`.badge`, `.dot`**: a count, and the smallest mark of a state.
- **`.skeleton`**: a surface's real structure, shimmering; `.cc-block` for anything that draws itself.
- **`.icon`**: sized from `--icon-size`, stroked from `--icon-stroke`.

## What it owns

Its own variables, named after it: `--tag-h`, `--tag-color`, `--btn-h`, `--btn-fs`, `--av-n`, `--input-h`, `--tab-h`, `--badge-h`. A kind, a size, a state or a context sets those, never the properties directly. A control is never shorter than its own type: above the cell's cap it grows by whole cells from its own height.

## How it meets the line

- **Stand:** h1 to h3. Their line height rounds to whole cells and every baseline lands on a cell line.
- **Hang:** every other type role. The top of the capitals sits on a line and the block rounds up to whole cells.
- **Inset:** a control's padding and a tag's padding sit inside its edge, in half cells. A control is one thing, like a word: its edges stay on lines and its contents keep their own rhythm.
- **Center:** an icon beside capitals centers on the cap height, beside lowercase on the x-height. `--size-icon` is one cell plus a line each side, so an icon centered on a cell is even.

## Rules it keeps

- R7, R8, R9: center only what differs by an even number of cells; text beside a control centers its capitals on it; an icon centers on the cap height or the x-height.
- R10: marks never stretch a line.
- R11: truncate sideways only.
- R12: overhangs are given back.
- R13, R14: one concept, one primitive; products turn knobs.
- R17: control type is fixed and listed, and a control is never shorter than its own type.
- R18: on touch every control's hit area is at least `--hit-min`.
