# M5 · Center

## What it is

A box straddles a line by the same amount either side: icons, dots, nodes on the lattice. It is the one relation where a line runs through the thing rather than along its edge.

## Its parts

- **The line** it straddles: a cell line, or a text's cap height or x-height.
- **An even size:** a box centered on a line overhangs it equally, so its size is even against the cells. `--size-icon` is one cell plus a line each side (14 at a 12px cell); an 18 box is a cell plus a quarter cell each side.

## What it owns

Its own size, chosen so the overhang is equal. Nothing else: a centered box is placed by what it centers on.

## How it meets the line

Across: the line passes through the box's middle.

Examples: an icon beside capitals centers on the cap height, beside lowercase on the x-height (`.snap-x`). A label beside a control centers its capitals on the control (`.cap-center`). A node on the 2:1 isometric lattice sits on a lattice point, which is also a cell corner.

Controlled by: no knob. The size tokens (`--size-icon`, `--size-icon-sm`, `--size-icon-md`, `--size-icon-lg`) and the trims (`.snap-x`, `.cap-center`) carry it.

## Rules it keeps

- R1: a box centered on a line is even.
- R7: center only what differs by an even number of cells; otherwise align to the start.
- R8: beside a control, text centers its capitals on it. This assumes one line; a cap-centered label that wraps is an open item.
- R9: an icon centers on the cap height or the x-height.
- R12: an icon taller than its line gives its overhang back.
