# A1 · Page

## What it is

The outermost level: the field of cells the whole page sits on. It decides how many cells there are and where the grid's strongest lines fall. Everything below it inherits that.

## Its parts

- **`.frame`**: a centered box whose width is whole modules of 4 cells, plus the hair its last line needs. Its inline padding is the gutter (`--gutter`), so the content inside it is the **field**. On a wide screen it steps by a module up to `--frame-max` (100 cells by default); the margins take the rest.
- **`.shell`**: navigation, main and an aside, for an app. `.shell` is the container and `.shell-grid` inside it does the layout, because a container query cannot restyle the container it asks. Parts: `.shell-grid`, `.shell-nav`, `.shell-main`, `.shell-aside`, `.shell-link`. Under 48rem of its own width the nav becomes a tab bar; under 72rem the aside moves under main.

## What it owns

- The fit: on a touch screen the screen's edge is the frame, and the cell is computed so whole cells span it (`--cells`, `--u`).
- The gutter: 2 or more cells either side, so the field stays whole modules (the gutter parity rule).
- The grid's strongest lines: the field's edges and every fourth line in from them.
- The shell's variables: `--shell-h`, `--shell-nav-w`, `--shell-aside-w`, `--shell-pad`.

The frame paints nothing. The shell's nav paints its own ground and a line, which makes the nav a surface inside the page level, not the page itself.

## How it meets the line

- **Inset:** the field sits a whole number of cells (the gutter) inside the frame.
- **Outset:** the grid bleeds past the frame to the screen's edges, and so may a band's color (F5).
- **Center:** the frame is centered on the screen; on a fitted screen the cell count is even, so the screen's center is a line.

## Rules it keeps

- R1: the grid fits whatever frames the page.
- R4: the grid is drawn in device pixels.
- R5: gutters are at least two cells, so a card that steps out never reaches the screen's edge.
- R19: above the cell's cap, layouts reflow because their breakpoints are in rem.
- F5: content in the field, color to the edge.
