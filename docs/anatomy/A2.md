# A2 · Section

## What it is

A band of the page: a head that introduces and a body that holds the work.

## Its parts

- **`.section`**: a grid of `.section-head` (a label, a title, a line of intro) and `.section-body`. Head and body are stacks: the head's gap is 1 cell, the body's 3.
- **`.cc-split`**: the head sits beside the body, `--section-head-w` wide (22 cells by default), when the container is wider than 54rem.
- **`.cc-band`**: the section's surface reaches the screen's edges while its content stays in the field. It is painted by an outset border image, so it never widens the page or adds sideways scroll. `--band-bg` sets its color.
- **`.cover`**: a full-screen opening. `.cover-media` reaches the screen's edges, `.cover-body` keeps the words in the field. Its height is whole cells (`--cover-h`), and `--cover-scrim` darkens the media under the words.

## What it owns

The space between its head and body (2 cells down, 4 across), and in a band, its block padding (6 cells) and its color. A plain section paints nothing. A band paints a ground, which a section may do without being a surface (D73). A cover's media is an element treated as a surface (D72).

## How it meets the line

- **Stand:** a section title in h1 to h3 stands on the cells.
- **Hang:** the label and the intro hang.
- **Inset:** a band's content sits 6 cells inside its block edges.
- **Outset:** a band's color and a cover's media reach past the field to the screen's edges.

## Rules it keeps

- R1, R6: its parts start on lines and the space between them is whole cells.
- R15: `.cc-split` and `.cc-band` change variables and layout, never markup.
- F5: content in the field, color to the edge.
