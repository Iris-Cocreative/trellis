# M2 · Hang

## What it is

The top of the capitals sits on a cell line, and the block rounds up to whole cells. All other text hangs: `.t-h4` to `.t-h6`, `.t-xl`, `.t-lg`, `.t-body`, `.t-sm`, `.t-xs`, `.t-label`, `.t-eyebrow`, and the matching tags in `.prose`.

Through 0.9, the word also named a card stepping out sideways. That is now Outset (M4), so Hang means only this.

## Its parts

- **Its own line height**, proportional and never forced to the grid: `1em × --t-lh`.
- **The box**, trimmed to the capitals at the top, so the capitals touch the line.
- **The block's height**, rounded up to whole cells. Browsers with `calc-size()` do this in CSS; elsewhere (Safari today) `settle.js` counts the cells the block needs and writes the count to `--settle-n`, which core makes its least height.

## What it owns

The rounding of its own block, so whatever follows starts on a line. A wrapping row (`.cluster`) hangs too, but rounds to the nearest cell, halves down, or up at the end of a surface.

## How it meets the line

From above: the capitals hang from the line like washing from a rope, and the lines below keep their own rhythm.

Example: the body paragraph in the specimen's "Headlines stand, text hangs" section says so of itself; the next block starts on a line however many lines it wraps to.

Controlled by: `--body-min`, `--body-max`, `--rp-min`, `--rp-max` (the size and the paragraph ratio), `--lh-body`, `--lh-p-step` and `--lh-label` (the line ratios). All are knobs. `math.js` has it as `hang()` and `settleCluster()`.

## Rules it keeps

- R1: edges land on lines.
- R6: between blocks, space is whole cells.
- R10: marks never stretch a line.
- R11: truncate sideways only; a trimmed box ends at the baseline.
