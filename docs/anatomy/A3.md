# A3 · Composition

## What it is

How a region arranges what it holds. A composition owns the gap between its children and paints nothing.

## Its parts

| Class | What it does | Its variables |
|---|---|---|
| `.stack` | one above another | `--stack-gap` (1 cell) |
| `.cluster` | a wrapping row: tags, chips, buttons | `--cluster-gap` (½ cell) |
| `.cols` | n equal columns, each rounded down to whole cells | `--cols`, `--cols-gap` |
| `.split` | two columns at a ratio, 2 : 1 by default; one column under 36rem of container | `--split-a`, `--split-b`, `--split-gap` |
| `.grid` | as many fixed tracks as fit; tracks don't stretch | `--grid-min`, `--grid-gap` |
| `.cells` | the lattice as a layout: children placed and sized in cells | `--cell-x`, `--cell-y`, `--cell-w`, `--cell-h` |
| `.rail` | a sideways strip that scrolls and snaps | `--rail-item`, `--rail-gap` |
| `.row`, `.col`, `.context` | a wrapping row of flexible columns; `.context` makes any box a container | `--row-gap` |
| `.list` | rows divided by hairlines: chats, actions, members, post indexes | `--list-pad-y`, `--list-gap`, `--list-rule`, `--list-outset` |
| `.board` | lanes of cards in a sideways scroller | `--board-lane-w`, `--board-gap`, `--board-lane-gap` |
| `.prose` | bare tags from an editor, each mapped to its role | `--prose-inner-gap`, `--stack-gap`, `--embed-w`, `--embed-h` |

## What it owns

The gap, the tracks and the count. Nothing else: no ground, no edge, no corner. Two compositions come close to painting, and each draws only what keeps its rows apart:

- `.list` draws a hairline after each row, a plane after its bottom coordinate that adds no height, and a hover ground on a row that links.
- `.prose` gives a `pre` a ground and padding. That `pre` is a surface inside the composition (A4), not the composition painting.

## How it meets the line

- **Hang:** a cluster rounds its height to the nearest cell, halves down, or up at the end of a surface (D55). Inside it, things keep their own half-cell rhythm, like letters in a word.
- **Outset:** a row that holds nothing but cards steps out as a whole. `.list` rows step out by `--list-outset`, so the hover ground reaches past the text while the text stays on the column's edge.

A composition never stands, insets or centers. It places its children on lines and lets them meet the line themselves.

## Rules it keeps

- R2: columns round to cells.
- R5: a row steps out only when it holds nothing but cards.
- R6: between blocks, space is whole cells; half-cell gaps reach clusters only.
- R15: a context changes a composition's variables, never its markup.
- F1, F3, F4: free arrangements rest on the lattice, live in their own layer (`.cells`) and are written in spans.
