# A4 · Surface

## What it is

A plane that paints. A surface owns a ground, an edge, a corner and the padding inside, and it is a container, so its type answers to its own width.

## Its parts

- **`.card`**: the surface on the page. Padding from `--card-pad-inline` and `--card-pad-block` (both follow `--density-card-pad` unless a product splits them; `--card-pad` sets both). Corner from `--card-radius` (`--shape-card`), edge color from `--card-border`. `.card-media` inside it takes a corner concentric with the card's, from the nearer padding.
- **`.float`**: one surface for every popover, options card and tooltip (`.options-card`, `.tooltip`). Top layer, at a fixed position; the app places it by its anchor (R16). Variables: `--float-pad`, `--float-radius`.
- **`.modal`**: the native `<dialog>`, as `.modal > .modal-inner > .modal-header`, `.modal-body`, `.modal-footer`. `.modal-inner` is the surface. `.modal.cc-tray` is the same parts sliding in from a side, flush to its edge with its ends inset by `--tray-margin`: left by default, or `.cc-right`, `.cc-top`, `.cc-bottom` (D77). Variables: `--modal-w`, `--modal-pad`, `--modal-gutter`, `--modal-radius`, `--modal-scrim`, `--tray-w`, `--tray-margin`.
- **The `pre` in `.prose`**: a code sample with one cell of padding and a sunken ground.

`.member-tile` is a card with a person in it; `.chat-item-bubble` is a small surface inside a chat item.

## What it owns

- **A ground:** `--surface`, which it sets for itself (a card's is `--bg-raised`), so what sits on it can read the ground it is on.
- **An edge:** a card draws its edge as an `::after` frame one line larger, inside the box on the left and top and just outside it on the right and bottom, so each edge lies on its grid line's pixels and the box never changes size. A float and a modal use an inset outline instead, because they align to an anchor or the screen, not the grid: a stated exception to R3 (D75).
- **A corner**, and nested corners stay concentric.
- **The padding inside.**
- **A container:** `.card`, `.modal-inner`, the options card and the `pre` in prose set `container-type: inline-size`, so `.t-h3` in a narrow card is smaller than `.t-h3` on the page. A surface that sizes to its content (a tooltip, a chat bubble) need not be a container (D74).

## How it meets the line

- **Inset:** its content sits whole cells inside its edge (the padding).
- **Outset:** a card's edge steps `--outset` cells out into the space around it, so its text lines up with the text beside it.
- **Stand and Hang:** the type inside it, by its own role.
- A float meets no line: it centers on its anchor (R16).

## Rules it keeps

- R3: a surface's edge is a plane after its coordinate and never changes its size.
- R5: surfaces step out; outset is clipped by an ancestor with hidden overflow.
- R13: one concept, one primitive: one card, one float, one modal.
- R15: a context changes a surface's variables: `.cc-dense .card { --card-pad: u }`.
- R16: floating surfaces align to their anchor, not the page grid.
