# D28. Free work may borrow the trellis

## Context

Organic interfaces (maps, constellations, globes, 3D) are not gridded, and the rules had cut them off from the grid entirely.

## Options considered

Not recorded. The sources say what was chosen, not what else was weighed. Flagged for the owner.

## Decision

Free work may still use the trellis. A globe can sit on a composition's center point, a card on a globe keeps the rules inside itself as it zooms, and a 3D surface can use a 3D version of the grid.

## Consequences

Work off the grid declares which relation it has (on it, from it, or free in a frame) and what it keeps from the seed. The README's organic interfaces section does not say this yet.
