# D56. A heading's own space rounds up

## Context

A heading's own space is its size times its share (`--t-mb`), settled on the cells, and in a stack only what exceeds the gap shows (D14). Rounded to the nearest cell it dropped a cell at common sizes: the specimen's h3 at 1440 needed 17.99px, rounded to one 12px cell, equal to the 1-cell gap, and got nothing. In a 1-cell stack no heading had space of its own, and in a 3-cell section body none ever did.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: retune the shares so most headings land on 2 cells; round the space up; amend D14 to the larger of the space and the gap plus one cell (breaking, and stacks would need a space before headings); or sum the space and the gap (reverting D14, breaking).

## Decision

A heading's own space rounds up to whole cells. D14 holds: in a stack it is still the larger of that space and the gap.

## Consequences

Most headings gain a cell after them in a 1-cell stack. `math.js`'s `spaceAfter()` rounds up, and `tests/math.js` checks a heading's real margin against it. The rhythm model is unchanged: its values moved, its rules did not.
