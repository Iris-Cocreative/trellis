# D46. Cap the cell at 1.5

## Context

The cell follows the reader's text size. Accessibility sizes on iOS and Android reach about three times normal, which would turn a 40-cell phone into a 13-cell phone.

## Options considered

No cap, or a cap above which only type grows and layouts reflow. The cap.

The number was not weighed. The review session proposed 1.5, and it was accepted as proposed. No other value, such as 1.25 or 2, was tested.

## Decision

The cell follows the reader's text size up to 1.5 times the default and no further. Above that only type grows.

## Consequences

Rule R19. `text-scale-max` in `tokens.json`, `cellScale()` in `math.js`. Controls past the cap needed their own answer (D52), and WebKit's reading of the cell its own fix (D53).
