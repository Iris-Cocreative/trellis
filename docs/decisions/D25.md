# D25. The modal is the native dialog

## Context

The apps built their modals by hand, with a scripted manager.

## Options considered

A scripted modal manager: it builds by hand what the dialog gives. The native `<dialog>`, opened with `showModal()`: chosen.

## Decision

One `.modal` archetype on the native dialog, with `.cc-sheet` as a variant. On a screen under 48rem every modal is a sheet.

## Consequences

The top layer, focus trap, Escape and inert page come free. The scroll lock is `html:has(.modal[open])`. A backdrop click is the app's one line of script.
