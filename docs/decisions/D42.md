# D42. Card padding in two knobs, equal by default

## Context

Designers usually give controls more horizontal than vertical padding, because untrimmed text carries hidden vertical space. Trellis trims text to its capitals and its baseline.

## Options considered

Unequal padding by default, as designers usually draw it, or equal by default because trimmed text makes equal padding optically equal. Equal by default, with a knob to split it: chosen.

## Decision

Two knobs, `--density-card-pad-inline` and `--density-card-pad-block`, both following `--density-card-pad`. On a card, `--card-pad-inline` and `--card-pad-block`; `--card-pad` is the shorthand a variant sets for both.

## Consequences

Breaking: `--card-pad` is no longer defined on every card. A product may tighten the sides and keep the vertical rhythm. Padding should also clear the corner curve; that is its own item.
