# D71. F6 moves to the plan for scenes

## Context

F6 said scroll scenes use CSS scroll-driven animation, not scroll listeners. Nothing in Trellis used a scroll timeline, and `.scene` was named and not built, so the rule had no example and no test.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, keep it as a rule, questioned until `.scene` is built; B, move it out of the rules into the plan for `.scene` and Motion proportions until something uses it; C, withdraw it.

## Decision

B. F6 leaves the rules. Its page is marked withdrawn and keeps its ID; the guidance moves to the plan for `.scene` and Motion proportions, where it returns as a rule when something uses it.

## Consequences

The README's free rules keep number 6 and say where it went. No code changes.
