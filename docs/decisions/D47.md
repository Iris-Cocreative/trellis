# D47. Person colors: no change for now

## Context

Giving every member of the first app a color would make the avatar rule identical in every app, and remove a fork in how group avatars are ringed.

## Options considered

Give members a color, or keep the current avatar color rules.

## Decision

Keep the current avatar color rules for now. It may be adjusted later.

## Consequences

The avatar rule keeps its fork between the two apps.

2026-11-11

The owner.
