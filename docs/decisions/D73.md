# D73. A section or a page may paint a ground

## Context

`.section.cc-band` paints a full-bleed band through a border image, and `.shell-nav` paints a ground and a line. Neither is a container, so by the definition neither is a surface, yet both paint; the pages called them a section or page with a surface of its own.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, a section or a page may paint a ground without being a surface, and the definition says so; B, they become surfaces, each a container so the type inside answers to its width.

## Decision

A. A section or a page may paint a ground, and a line where it meets the next region, without being a surface. It owns no corner and no padding of its own as a surface does, and it isn't a container.

## Consequences

The anatomy pages say so (A1, A2 and the definitions); no code changes.
