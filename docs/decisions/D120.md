# D120. A fill's state moves away from its label

## Context

The shared state layer (D58) mixes a control's own ink into its own fill: 6% hovered, 12% pressed, 10% more when selected. On a fill with a white label that lightens the fill, and the label loses contrast as the state deepens. Measured from the pixels in Chromium, light theme, a primary button is 4.88:1 at rest, 4.35 hovered and 3.89 pressed; confirm 4.72, 4.24 and 3.82; destructive 5.46, 4.81 and 4.30. A selected filled button was under 4.5 at rest (a selected primary 4.04, confirm 3.94), and every solid tag that can be pressed fell under it pressed, the group kind to 3.88. D115 weighed darkening these kinds, kept one layer and set the floor while pressed at 3:1.

On 2026-11-27 the owner decided that a filled control whose label is white darkens on hover and press, so the label holds 4.5:1 in every state in both themes, by one rule rather than one per primitive: the layer's direction follows the ink. Nothing at rest moves; outline, ghost and quiet kinds and fills with a dark label keep what they do unless they fail too.

## Options considered

Each was worked out for core, both example layers and every scope of the study, in both themes, at every share a control takes (6, 12, 10, 16 and 22%), and checked against what the browsers draw.

**Black under a white label, the ink under a dark one.** It holds every white label, and leaves the dark ones as they were. Those fail too: 52 states under 4.5, a selected destructive button pressed in core's night (4.47), every solid tag pressed in the study's grove at night (down to 4.01) and its lantern's buttons by day (3.38). So dark labels move as well.

**Away from the label on every fill.** Black under a white label, white under a dark one: no state under 4.5 anywhere. But a fill near white or near black has nothing to move into. A selected segment, a reaction and a pale stage are white or nearly, and a hovered one would mix white into white and show nothing.

**Away from the label near the flip, the ink elsewhere (chosen).** Where a fill is far from the flip its label has room, and its own ink is what shows. The band's edges are where the ink stops being safe at the largest share, 22%. Under .4 a white label still reads at 5.02:1 or more with 22% of white in its fill, at any hue and chroma inside sRGB; over .75 a dark label (the computed .16, or the text's .213 by day) reads at 4.67 or more with 22% of itself. The grounds sit well outside: under .28 at night and over .94 by day, in core, the examples and every scope. Core's own colored fills are all inside, from .51 to .72; of the layers', the study's darkest accent (.33) and its night colors and the examples' palest (.75 to .83) are outside, and read on their own ink. No state is under 4.5.

**A rule per primitive** (a darker hover on each kind, a solid tag's own press): what the owner asked not to do.

**A stronger share for the way out.** Black in a dark fill moves it less than white in it would: core's kinds at night (.71) now step 0.019 in OKLab when hovered, where they stepped 0.034 toward their ink. A second set of shares would make the two directions match, and three knobs six. Kept to the three; each step is still held at 0.01 or more, and products turn the knobs.

## Decision

- Every control with a fill of its own names its label's ink and its fill: `--state-ink` and `--state-fill`, the names `.u-state` already took, now registered so they do not inherit. A button, a tag, a tab, a reaction and `.u-state` do.
- One rule works out what the layer mixes in, `--state-tone`, for all of them. On an opaque fill between .4 and .75 of lightness it is black where the fill is under the flip (a white label) and white where it is over (a dark label). Anywhere else, on a tint and on no fill, it is the ink, as before.
- Each primitive paints `color-mix(in oklab, var(--state-tone) <share>, <its fill>)`. At rest the tone is the ink itself, so nothing at rest is drawn otherwise, to the last digit.
- CSS cannot compare two colors, so the choice is made with alpha: the fill gives black or white at full alpha in the band and none outside it, 99.99% of that is mixed with the ink, which keeps whichever has any alpha, and the alpha is restored. A browser without relative colors keeps the ink.
- `math.js` says the same (`stateOver`, `STATE_BAND`). `tools/product.js` holds the label of a primary, destructive and confirm button and a button in a color of the product's own at rest, hovered, pressed, selected, selected and hovered, and selected and pressed, and a solid tag in every kind, status, stage and the neutral hovered and pressed, at 4.5:1, for every layer and scope, at the layer's own shares. `tests/contract.js` holds that for core, every layer and scope and every accent across sRGB, and that core.css's band and paint are math.js's. `tests/layout.js` reads each from the pixels in both engines and themes, with the pointer, and holds each state 0.01 in OKLab from the one before it.

## Consequences

- By day a primary button is 4.88:1 at rest, 5.58 hovered and 6.43 pressed; confirm 4.72, 5.44 and 6.29; destructive 5.46, 6.22 and 7.15. At night their labels are dark and their fills lighten: 7.72, 8.24 and 8.71 for the primary. The lowest label of any filled control, in any state, is its rest.
- A selected filled button is a little darker by day (a selected primary 6.13:1, was 4.04) and lighter at night. It was the one state under 4.5 before anything was pressed.
- Fills with a dark label inside the band lighten now where they darkened: core's accent, kinds and statuses at night, and by day a light accent or a stage between the flip and .75. A paler fill keeps darkening into its ink.
- Unchanged: plain, ghost and outline buttons, tinted and quiet tags, tabs, a selected segment, reactions, list rows, menu rows. The switch and the choice mix the text's ink into their fill; in core the text sits across the flip from the accent in both themes, so a checked box already darkens by day and lightens at night, and its mark is a non-text 3:1. The own chat bubble takes no state.
- A product that turns `--state-press` up is checked at its own share, and is named if a label falls under.
