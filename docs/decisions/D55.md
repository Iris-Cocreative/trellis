# D55. A cluster at a surface's end rounds up

## Context

A cluster rounds to the nearest cell, halves down (D19), so a row of 1.5-cell tags takes one cell and overflows half a cell into the gap after it. R6 allows that because at least a cell follows. At the end of a surface nothing follows but the surface's own padding, and the overflow ate half of it: a dense card looked squashed at the bottom, and so did the dense member card's button row.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: a cluster at the end of a surface rounds up, as a clause on D19 (additive); every cluster rounds up again, reverting D19 (breaking, it changes rhythm everywhere); or dense tags at 2 cells (fixes the symptom, loses density).

## Decision

The last cluster in a card, a modal body or a member tile rounds up to whole cells, in CSS and in `settle.js`. Everywhere else D19 holds.

## Consequences

D19 gains a clause. `math.js`'s `settleCluster()` takes `atEnd`. A dense card is a cell taller where its last row overflowed; nothing else moves.
