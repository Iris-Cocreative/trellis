# D79. Proportions: the tuner now, the rest when it shows a need

## Context

The owner asked for the type ratio to become a chosen proportion that shows up in space as whole-cell counts (Fibonacci for golden), with a tuner and optional clamps that answer to the screen or the container. The proportion was already in core, unnamed: the space ladder is the root-two series, and type uses two ratios with nothing tying them to space. Type answers only to its container and is always clamped, and the specimen's type list was static markup.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, stage 1, the tuner on today's knobs: the specimen as knob editor, a named proportion writing the type ratios, density, fine tuning, a clamp graph per role, a type list generated live from `math.js`, a resizable box, and export of one product rule, with no core change; B, A plus two knobs, `--t-axis` (type answers to the container or the screen) and `--t-clamp` (clamped, or continuing past the ends); C, stage 2, a count series as tokens per named proportion, primitives moving to steps, the seed becoming "a cell and a proportion", breaking; D, stage 3, the four-cell module follows the proportion.

## Decision

A now, B when the tuner shows the need, C only after the first app runs on Trellis (D36). D stays closed. The specimen's Tune it section: a named proportion writes the four type ratios (headings by the proportion wide and its two-thirds power narrow, paragraphs by its two-thirds power wide and its 0.4 power narrow, which at a perfect fourth is the neutral seed), a density, fine tuning of every type knob, a graph of every role's size against its container's width from `math.js`, a box to resize whose roles are the real classes checked against `math.js`, and the knobs exported as one product layer, copied or saved as `product.css`. The proportion's preferred cell counts are shown and not applied.

## Consequences

No core change. "Specimen as knob editor" is done: the export works. The tuner sets its knobs on its own box unless asked to apply them to the whole page. B, C and D wait.
