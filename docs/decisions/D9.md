# D9. Lines are planes

## Context

A line cannot be infinitely thin: it occupies pixels.

## Options considered

Not recorded. The sources say what was chosen, not what else was weighed. Flagged for the owner.

## Decision

A line occupies the pixels just after its coordinate, right of it and below it. N cells drawn with their edges need N cells plus one line. `--hair` is one device pixel.

## Consequences

Rule R1 and R3. The fit subtracts a hair from the width so the last line has its pixel. `tests/pixel.js` checks card edges at the device pixel.
