# D32. Fold keeps text where it lives

## Context

Links that expand in place (an idea borrowed, not code) can pull a section from the same page or another.

## Options considered

Not recorded. The sources say what was chosen, not what else was weighed. Flagged for the owner.

## Decision

Fold is built as a prose primitive on native disclosure elements, and the referenced text stays where it lives. Mentions expand the same way into a small kind card.

## Consequences

Pulling from another site needs that site to allow it. Not built.

## Date

2026-11-10. The plan records the choice on this date; the day it was made may be earlier.

## Who

The owner.

## Superseded by

None.
