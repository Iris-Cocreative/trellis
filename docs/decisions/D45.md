# D45. Five ways to meet a line

## Context

Stand and Hang described type. Hang also named a card stepping out sideways, so one word meant two things, and boxes had no names for how they meet a line.

## Options considered

Say yes to the proposal, say no, or name the relations otherwise. Yes.

## Decision

Five ways anything meets a line: Stand (a last baseline on a line), Hang (a cap top on a line), Inset (content a step inside an edge), Outset (an edge a step outside a line) and Center (straddling a line). The card behavior called Hang becomes Outset.

## Consequences

Breaking: `--hang` is now `--outset` and `--list-hang` is now `--list-outset`, with no alias. Applied in Apply the five relations.
