# D18. Cards step out a cell

## Context

A card with a one-cell padding pushes its text a cell in from the text outside it.

## Options considered

Not recorded. The sources say what was chosen, not what else was weighed. Flagged for the owner.

## Decision

A card steps a product knob's worth of cells (1 by default) out into the space around it, so its text lines up with the text outside. A row steps out only when it is all cards; a card in a column stays put; nested cards step into their parent's padding. It was called Hang, with the knob `--hang`.

## Consequences

Rule R5. Any ancestor with hidden overflow clips it (written down in 0.7.0). `.list` rows and a `pre` in prose follow it.

2026-11-02

Not recorded. It shipped in 0.6.0.
