# D20. Motion is transform and opacity only

## Context

Free, flowing and moving layouts needed rules that keep them on the cells.

## Options considered

Not weighed at the time. Options named since: no rule at all; transform and opacity by default, with layout motion as a stated exception. D27 records what replaced it.

## Decision

The first free-layout rule said motion is transform and opacity only, so it never reflows.

## Consequences

Motion could never move layout.

## Date

2026-11-02

## Who

Not recorded. It shipped in 0.6.0.
