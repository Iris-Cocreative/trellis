# D1. One seed for every product

## Context

An audit of two working apps found dozens of font sizes, scores of spacing values and radii, thousands of one-off utility values, and many separate builds of the same avatar, modal and pill. Nothing stopped the drift.

## Options considered

Two options were weighed. A utility framework on shared CSS-variable tokens: one of the two apps already ran one, and the draft plan recommended it. Or a named-class system in plain modern CSS: chosen.

The owner reversed the draft. Under the utility framework the audit found thousands of one-off bracketed values across hundreds of files. A project-wide tweak there means editing hundreds of files, and relationships between elements have nowhere to live. Shared token names, not shared machinery, are what let one app's parts render in the other.

## Decision

Build one shared seed for both apps: a unit of space (0.75rem, rounded to an even pixel) and a ratio for type, with a core stylesheet and a product layer per app.

## Consequences

Values become relationships. Every later rule is a statement about the unit or the ratio. The unit became the cell (D5, D13).

## Date

2026-10-28

## Who

The owner, reversing a draft recommendation. It shipped in 0.2.0.

## Superseded by

None. The unit was refined by D5 and D13.
