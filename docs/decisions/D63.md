# D63. A board pads its cards a cell

## Context

A board's lane cards took the default two-cell card padding and the lane head had no inset, so the lane's label sat on the cards' edge while their text was two cells in. Cards in a lane don't step out, which is right inside a scroller.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, a `--board-card-pad` (a cell) for the lane cards' padding and the lane head's inline padding, so label and card text share a line; B, Outset in lanes, cards stepping out of the lane and the board stepping out like a list, which would close the two-cell lane gap and need it to grow to four.

## Decision

A. `.board` sets `--board-card-pad` to a cell. A card in a lane pads by it, and the lane head pads its sides by it, so the lane's label and its cards' text share a line.

## Consequences

An additive variable; a visual change to every board. The lane gap stays two cells. A product or context can widen it back with the one variable.
