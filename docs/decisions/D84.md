# D84. Measures a compact app needs are variables, not a second scale

## Context

Agent Weaver, a desktop tool with 24px controls and 12px rows, moved its Sessions panel onto Trellis as a trial of compact density (its ledger, Request 1). Tokens carried everything the app draws itself. Two primitives did not fit, because the measure that mattered was written into the rule and not a variable. A segmented control could not go under 30px: its tabs take `--tab-h`, but the ring around them was half the difference between the large and the small control, so beside a 24px search box it stood 6px taller. An options card's items were 2½ cells tall in the 14px control type, and the card 16 cells wide and at most 24 tall: a nine-row menu lost its group headings to fit, and its type read too large beside 12px rows. Separately, a tooltip's ink was `color: var(--bg)`, so a tooltip on any ground but the page's ink could not be reached by setting `--surface`.

## Options considered

A compact context, `.cc-dense` reaching both primitives with a tighter set of measures. It would change every dense container that already holds a segmented control or a menu, in products that did not ask, and it answers one density where an app may want another.

The variables themselves, each defaulting to what was fixed. Nothing moves until a context sets one, and an app picks its own measures from the tokens.

Leaving it, and letting the app restyle. That is the mistake the rules exist to prevent.

For the ink: a tooltip variable, or one on every float. A float already takes its ground from `--surface`; its ink belongs beside it.

## Decision

The owner judged the trial by eye and said go: variables, not a second scale and not a change to `.cc-dense`.

- `--seg-pad` on `.tabs.cc-seg`, the ring around its tabs. Default: half the difference between the large and the small control, as before.
- `--option-h` and `--option-fs` (an item's height and type) and `--options-w` and `--options-max-h` (the card's width and cap) on `.options-card`. Defaults: 2½ cells, the control type, 16 cells, 24 cells.
- `--float-ink` on `.float`, the page's ink by default; a tooltip sets `--surface` and `--float-ink` to the page's ink and the page.

## Consequences

- No default changes: the specimen renders as it did.
- A compact segmented control sets two variables together, `--tab-h` and `--seg-pad`. At 1½ cells and ¼ it is two cells tall and stays on the lines; other pairs can leave them, which is the app's to check.
- An options card's item height is the app's to keep in half cells, as the rule beside it says.
- `.cc-dense` still does not shrink a segmented control or an options card. If a second app asks for the same measures, that is the time to name a context for them.
- A variable is set on the primitive, in a context, not on its ancestor: the skill says so since `c5bb825`.
