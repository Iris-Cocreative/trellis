# D83. Ink on every solid fill, and marks that survive forced colors

## Context

Writing the Color and Theme chapter measured two gaps. A solid tag's label and a badge's count were fixed white, and four pairs missed 4.5:1: white on the person kind (3.80:1), the group kind and warn (4.44:1) and ok (4.16:1) in light, and a badge's count on the alert, lifted in dark (2.57:1). The contract did not check them. And in forced colors, current and selected marks, the switch and dots were drawn with a shadow or a fill alone, so the system removed them; the avatar's ring went the same way.

## Options considered

The fix for the ink was named with the finding: compute it from the fill, as the accent's is, and add the pairs to the contract. Measured, that was not enough at the default seeds. The group kind and warn were mid-tones where neither white nor the dark ink reached 4.5:1 (4.44 and 4.36; 4.44 and 4.38), and ok took white at 4.16 where the dark ink would have read. The ways out were a lower flip, a darker ink, or darker seeds. A lower flip moves the accent's ink and still leaves group and warn short; a darker ink changes every computed ink, the accent's with it. The smallest change was to the three seeds.

For forced colors: one block in system colors, or a note that the losses are accepted.

## Decision

The owner said go, on both. A solid tag and a badge compute their ink from their fill with the accent's formula. `--seed-group` moves from 0.563 to 0.555 lightness, `--seed-ok` from 0.571 to 0.547, `--seed-warn` from 0.578 to 0.572, hue and chroma unchanged: each is the lightest value where white reads at 4.5:1. The contract holds the ink on every solid kind and status at 4.5:1, in both themes, for core and every product layer.

One `@media (forced-colors: active)` block draws the lost marks again: current and selected in `Highlight`, the switch, dots, a badge's pill and an avatar's ring in `CanvasText`, with `forced-color-adjust: none` only where a mark is a background.

## Consequences

- The person kind's solid label is now a dark ink. The three kinds and statuses moved are a little darker everywhere they are used, tints included.
- A product whose seed sits in the middle band fails the contract and is named.
- `tests/layout.js` checks forced colors in both engines. C4 and C5 are settled.
