# D103. A ring is a dot not yet filled

## Context

The planning app draws a piece of work as a ring while it is an idea, planned or scheduled, and as a filled dot from underway on. `.dot` could only fill, and its fill is not a variable apart from `--dot-color`. Two apps drew the ring themselves, each with an inset shadow in the status color: the desktop tool's mention picker (its request 16) and the planning app's owed-cards bench (`.oc-ring`, its gap 2). The bench also needed a finer line on a half-cell bead, and wrote a second rule for it.

## Options considered

A variable for the dot's fill alone (`--dot-fill`), with each app drawing the line. The line is the part both apps had to write, and its width depends on the dot's size.

A new primitive beside the dot. One concept, one primitive (rule 13): a ring is the same mark in another state.

A ring drawn as an inset shadow, as both apps had it. A shadow is removed in forced colors, and a fill under it shows as a fringe outside the curve. A border is drawn in whole device pixels, so the line stays sharp at every size.

One fixed width of two lines. On a half-cell bead two lines leave a two-pixel hole, and the ring reads as a dot.

## Decision

- `.dot.cc-ring`: the dot's own box, with a border in `--dot-color` inside it and a clear inside. It adds no size.
- `--dot-ring` is the line: a quarter of the dot, one line at the least and two at the most. At the default cell that is a line and a half on a half-cell bead (one device pixel on a 1x screen) and two lines from three quarters of a cell up. A context may set it.
- `--dot-fill` is the inside, `transparent` by default, so a ring is right on any ground, a hovered row and a photograph included. On a line (a rail, a rule) set `--dot-fill: var(--surface)`, and the line stops at the ring.
- A quiet ring takes `--text-tertiary` where a quiet dot takes `--text-faint`: a line is thinner than a fill, and the faint ink is decoration with no contrast floor.
- In forced colors a ring is a `CanvasText` line around `Canvas`, so it stays apart from a filled dot.
- `tests/contract.js` holds each status at 3:1 on the page and on every raised level, in both themes, for core and every product layer. `tests/layout.js`, both engines: the box, the line at two sizes, both variables, forced colors.

## Consequences

A process can be drawn with one primitive: rings for what has not started, dots for what has. The bench and the desktop tool can drop their own rings.

A product layer whose accent sits close to its 3:1 floor may miss the new floor for a status, on the third raised level first. It sets that status seed.
