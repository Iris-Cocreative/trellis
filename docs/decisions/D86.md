# D86. Space is counted in cells, not doubled

## Context

The cell was described as something that "halves and doubles": the README and the seed said so, the space tokens were called the octaves, and the specimen drew them as a ladder of bars, each longer than the last. Halving and doubling is a ratio. Drawn that way, the cell read as a second scale beside the type ratio, and the two seeds were hard to tell apart. The owner called it out on the specimen: the cell is cellular, a doubling scale is not something to enforce on every product, and the drawing should show counts of cells with what each is used for, not a progression.

## Options considered

Keeping the framing. The tokens that exist (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, with 3, 6 and 12 between) do lean on doubling, and the words described them. But the words also made a rule out of a habit, and no check ever kept it.

Naming another scale as the default, such as the Fibonacci numbers. That swaps one enforced ratio for another.

Counting. Space is a count of cells and any whole count is on the grid. Halves and quarters stay inside things. A product may favor a set of counts, as it chooses a type ratio, and Trellis enforces no set.

## Decision

Space is counted in cells, not doubled.

- Any whole count of cells is on the grid. Between blocks, space is whole cells, as before (R6). Halves and quarters live inside things.
- The space tokens are named counts, not octaves. They are the counts with names, not the only ones allowed: any other is `calc(var(--u) * n)`.
- A product may favor a set of counts that suits its ratio. The Fibonacci counts (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) suit a golden type ratio, so margins, gaps and type share one proportion; doubling counts (1, 2, 4, 8, 16) are another set. `--sp-5` and `--sp-13` are added so the Fibonacci set is all tokens.
- The specimen draws each count as that many real cells on the page's own lines, with its name and its use, and shows the two lines (`--line`, `--hair`) apart from them, since a line is not a cell and never scales.

## Consequences

- No value changes. Two tokens are added; nothing is removed or renamed.
- The wording changes in the README, the seed pages (S1, S6, S7, P2), `tokens.json` and the comments of `core.css`. Earlier decision records keep the words of their time.
- Nothing checks which counts a product uses, and nothing should: `tools/check.js` already counts raw lengths, and a whole count of cells is not one.
- Principle P2 no longer says sizes step by doubling. It was never kept by a rule.
- Not decided here: a knob that names a product's favored set, so that density or the layout gaps could step along it. Open until a product asks.
- The specimen's opening card for the cell still draws a quarter, a half, one, two and four. It reads as doubling, and is left as it is until the opening is next revised.
