# D98. A paper that is not white, and an accent and an ink with a night of their own

## Context

The community app moving its whole palette onto Trellis (after D96 and D97) found two colors core could not draw. Its home space's paper is a linen, `#f4efe8`: lightness .954, where core's day ground is .973, nearly white. And its buttons at night are a pale sage, `#cbe7b9`, where core lifts the forest accent by `--color-lift` to a muted green. Raising `--color-lift` would reach the sage, but it lifts every kind and status with it.

## Options considered

A lightness seed for the ground. Seeds carry hue and chroma; the ladder carries lightness. Mixing the two would make a seed mean two things.

`--day-depth`, the day's counterpart of `--night-lift`, and a night seed for the accent, the counterpart of `--seed-night` for grounds. Both follow what is there, so nothing moves.

## Decision

- `--day-depth`: subtracted from the lightness of every ground and edge by day. 0 by default; .019 is that linen. Ink darkens with it three times as fast, as it lightens at night (D97).
- `--seed-ink-night`: the hue and chroma of text at night (forest ink by day, cream by night). It follows the ink, as `--seed-night` follows the ground.
- `--seed-accent-night`: the accent at night, drawn as given. It follows `--derived-accent-night`, the accent lifted by `--color-lift`, so a product that leaves it sees no change. Its ink is computed from it, as always.
- All four are knobs, per scope too. `math.js`, the contract and `tests/math.js` (a paper at .019, a sage night accent and a cream night ink, both themes) say the same.

## Consequences

From the community app's seeds Trellis now draws `#f4efe8` by day and `#cbe7b9` on its night buttons. Not settled: the community app's secondary text is a warm gray under a forest ink, and Trellis draws every text role from one ink seed (TRELLIS-LEDGER in the community app).
