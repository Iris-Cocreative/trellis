# D38. Its own public repository

## Context

The foundation lived in a folder of a larger repository, beside private material.

## Options considered

Three placements were weighed first: stay a folder of the larger repository until both apps had adopted it, which was the plan until 2026-11-11; a private repository of its own; or a public one. Public won because the specimen and stylesheets were already served publicly, a private dependency costs every consumer a token, and CI and the site are free on a public repository.

For the history, the review session had earlier advised carrying it over by a subtree split. That was dropped because the old commits carried private files. That left two options: keep two public commits, the first carrying one sentence that named a product, or squash them into one fresh commit with a force push. One fresh commit: chosen.

## Decision

Trellis moves to its own public repository, served as a site from it, starting from one fresh first commit. The old location is a pointer and redirects.

## Consequences

Commits are authored as the organization, so no person's name is in the public history. The replaced commits stay visible on the host to someone who has their exact address until its support purges them.
