# D117. A product's own colors, and a night deeper than core's

## Context

**Colors.** Core has seeds for what every product has: an accent, kinds, statuses, seven stages. Two products have colors beyond them. The membership app has three axes of an assessment, and alliances; it draws them as relative colors of core's kinds, by hand, and nothing checks them. The community app has a gold, written four times: a fill and a text color, by day and by night. The owner approved seeds a product names for itself (2026-11-24), with a fill, an ink and a tint in both themes, as stages have (D107).

How each reads them differs. The membership app picks a color per element, `style="--axis: …"` on a card, a meter, a tag. The community app reads one gold in some twenty rules, and other colors beside it.

**A night.** The planning app's draft layer wanted `--night-lift: -.012`, a night a little deeper than core's, and the check passed it: `tools/product.js` read the number with a pattern that has no minus sign, found none, and checked core's night instead of the product's. `--day-depth`, `--color-lift` and `--ink-flip` were read the same way, and the last two on the root alone, where core reads them on every scope.

## Options considered

A custom property's name can't be made from another in CSS, so `--seed-own-gold` can't give a `--gold-ink` on the root. What pure CSS allows:

**Numbered slots,** as the stages are: `--seed-own-1` to `-6`, with three roles each on the root and every scope. Read anywhere, several at once. But a number says nothing (a stage's is its place in an order; a gold has none), so every app would write its own names over the slots, again on every scope; a count must be chosen, and the two products would already use four and, with its other colors, six; every page and every scope carries eighteen declarations for products that name none; and the Figma file would carry them too.

**The roles written in the product layer,** by hand or by a tool. A layer holds knobs only; this would put arithmetic in it, a copy of core's that goes stale.

**A documented recipe,** three lines of relative color an app copies. The same copies, unchecked.

**CSS functions** (`@function`). Chromium only.

**A seed as an argument.** One rule in core works the roles out on any element that says which seed is its own, `--seed: var(--seed-own-gold)`. The names are the product's, and there are as many as it has. It is declared on no element that doesn't ask.

**A solid fill.** A stage's solid tag takes its fill, and for a middling color neither ink reads on it (4.27:1 at the worst). Choices: say so; refuse such a color in the check, although only its solid tag is at fault; or move the fill clear of the flip. Moving it at most .04 of lightness holds 4.5:1 for every color in sRGB.

**A button variant.** Not built: the owner approved other button kinds and said more wait. What a button needs is a fill and an ink that reads on it.

**The modifier's word.** `cc-own` was the first choice; `.chat-item.cc-own` already means your own message, and one word with two meanings misleads. `cc-seed` says what the element takes.

**A night under 0:** refuse it; allow it to a floor; or allow it and hold ink where it is, which changes core's arithmetic. The arithmetic is linear, so under 0 the ink dims as the grounds do, three times as fast.

## Decision

**A product layer names its own colors, `--seed-own-<name>`, as many as it has. An element takes one as its `--seed`, and core works its roles out there.**

- **Naming.** `--seed-own-gold: #c09642;` in the layer, on the root or a scope, in any color, `#rrggbb` or `oklch(l c h)`. The prefix keeps a product's names clear of core's, now and later, and keeps a misspelled seed of core's an error. A name can't end in `-ink`, `-solid` or `-tint`.
- **Using.** `.u-seed` on a part of the app's own, or `.cc-seed` on a `.tag` or a `.dot`, with `--seed: var(--seed-own-gold)` on it or on anything round it. It has:
  - `--seeded`, the fill, `--seeded-ink`, the ink, and `--seeded-tint`: a stage's, by the same arithmetic and the same numbers;
  - `--seeded-solid`, a fill a label stands on: the fill, no lighter than .54 where its label is white and no darker than .62 where it is dark; and `--seeded-solid-ink`, that label.
- **Tag, dot, button.** A `.tag.cc-seed` is tinted from the fill; solid or selected it takes the solid fill. A `.dot.cc-seed` is the fill, its ring the ink. A button takes the pair through the variables it has: `.my-gold:not(:disabled, [aria-disabled="true"]) { --btn-bg: var(--seeded-solid); --btn-border: var(--seeded-solid); --btn-ink: var(--seeded-solid-ink); }`.
- **Read anywhere, or two at once.** A custom property inherits, so an element that has a color can give it a name: `u-seed` on the root and on each scope's element, and `:root, [data-product] { --seed: var(--seed-own-gold); --gold: var(--seeded); --gold-text: var(--seeded-ink); }`. A part that needs two colors takes one's name from its parent and the other itself.
- **Scopes (D96).** A name set on the root carries in; one a scope sets is its own. The roles are worked out on the element, so they follow its scope and its theme. `--seed` is resolved where it is written: written outside a scope, it is the outside's color.
- **Unset,** `--seed` is the accent.
- **The check.** `tools/product.js` finds each one a layer names, on the root and on every scope, in the themes that scope is seen in, and holds its ink at 4.5:1 on the page and every raised level, and the label on its solid fill at 4.5:1. It must be a color: a `var()` in its place is refused, since nothing would be checked.

**`--night-lift` may be under 0, to -.025. `--day-depth`, `--color-lift` and `--ink-flip` may not.** Each must be a plain number, and is read with its sign, on the root and on every scope; anything else is refused by name.

- -.025, because the tertiary ink dims with the night: on the third raised ground it is 4.65:1 at core's night, 4.60 at -.012, 4.53 at -.025, and under 4.5 a little past -.03. Grounds keep their order down to there, no step under three quarters of core's least.
- Near black sRGB holds little color, so under 0 every ground and edge of the product's night must be inside it. A strong `--seed-night` is refused, with the grounds that left.
- By day a raised surface is white at 0; under 0 it would leave sRGB and its step over the page would go.
- Core's arithmetic is as it was.

## Consequences

Nothing core draws moves. A product that names no color pays one rule that matches nothing.

`math.js`: a seed named `own-…` gives `palette()` four roles; `ownSolid()` and `inGamut()` are new. `tests/contract.js` holds the ink, the solid label and, over the page and the first two raised levels, the ink on the tint at 4.5:1 for about 3,900 colors across sRGB in both themes, and shows the layer check finding, passing and refusing. `tests/math.js` checks the five roles in the browser, on scopes, and the night at -.012 and -.025; `tests/layout.js` the variants, the recipe and forced colors; both engines.

On the third raised level and in a well, words on a tint take `--text`: the ink on its own tint there is 4.3 to 4.4:1 at the worst color.

A product whose night is a dusk: an ink at night is .76, which reads on every ground of a night lifted by up to about .1. That is so of the stages too. Since stages (D107) and the statuses' floor (D103), core's own seeds fail the check from a lift of .11, where D97 said .15; the template and the README say .1 now.

A named color that must be another at night than its own, lifted, has no form (the accent has `--seed-accent-night`). Nobody has asked.

Figma: the file holds no product's own color, because no layer in the repository names one. A layer that does would add, for each name, its fill, ink, solid and solid ink in both themes to Product, and the same to Color.
