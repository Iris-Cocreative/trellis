# D14. A heading's own space is the larger, not the sum

## Context

A heading carries space after it, proportional to its size. In a stack there is also the stack's gap.

## Options considered

Add the two, or take the larger. The larger was chosen.

## Decision

In a stack, a heading's space after it is the larger of its own space and the gap, never the sum.

## Consequences

Part of the rhythm model. `math.js` has `spaceAfterInStack()`. Prose keeps the same rule.
