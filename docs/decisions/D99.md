# D99. The conversation pieces: a composer, flat rows, and what a message carries

## Context

The planning app built Messages on Trellis and found the primitives too basic: a labeled field with a pill button beside it, outlined bubbles, a solid accent slab for your own message. A bench drew two directions, and its owner chose flat rows (Slack, Linear). The bench built them from app classes, and its brief (`docs/TRELLIS-BRIEF-CONVERSATION.md`, in the planning app's repository) asked Trellis to take them in, so messages, the assistant's chat, comments, the live room, short posts and the membership app draw from one foundation.

## Options considered

**Half-cell gaps between a run's messages**, as the brief asked. Every text block hangs from a cell line and rounds up to whole cells, so a half-cell gap puts the next message off the lines, and `align.js` would fail it. It also isn't needed: a single trimmed line of small text is one cell, so a one-cell gap puts consecutive messages a paragraph's line apart, which already reads as one block. The run gap stays a cell (`--chat-run-gap`), and the row's hover fill is drawn half a gap out each way, not laid out, so the rows of a run meet.

**Two lines that belong together.** A `.stack` holds them a cell apart. The brief asked for a half cell. A pair is like a cluster: inside, it keeps a half-cell rhythm, as letters do in a word, and its block rounds up to whole cells, so what follows is on a line. `.pair` is a composition, with `.chat-quote`, `.chat-link` and `.chat-reactions` rounding the same way.

**The composer's hint**, a `.composer-hint` inside the box's form. The box's edge would have to skip the hint, which needs a wrapper or a grid that knows how many controls there are. A `.field` already holds a box and a hint that starts at the box's text (`--field-inset`), and already settles. The composer goes in a `.field`, and the field moves its inset to the composer's text.

**The hover time**, absolutely placed in the avatar's column (the bench's, which crowded the avatar). It is a flex item in the avatar's place instead: a continuing message that holds a `.chat-item-time` drops its hidden avatar and the time takes the column's width, clipped to it, baseline-aligned with the text.

## Decision

- `.composer`: one box, `.composer-input` (a textarea, `field-sizing: content`; `settle.js` writes `--composer-h` where that is missing), `.composer-send` that wakes with text through `:placeholder-shown`. Three cells for one line, two more for each line after, up to `--composer-max`. It takes the focus ring. In a `.field`, its hint starts at the text and is gone on a coarse pointer, since it names keys.
- `.chat-thread`, and `.cc-flat` on it (or on one item): everyone at the start, a three-cell plain avatar, the name strong and the time on one baseline, `.chat-item-time` in the avatar's column on hover and focus, the row filled on hover, `.chat-item-actions` on its top edge. `.cc-own` changes only the name. The bubble's own-run meta rules don't apply to a flat thread.
- Parts drawn the same in both: `.chat-quote`, `.chat-file`, `.chat-link`, `.chat-reactions` > `.chat-reaction` (`.cc-mine` or `aria-pressed`, the emoji in `--font-emoji` at the large control size), `.chat-item.cc-removed`, `.chat-edited`, `.chat-divider` (`.cc-new`), `.chat-typing` (from `--chat-inset`, the text's column; the dots still under reduced motion). Nothing in a thread shrinks, so a scroller never clips its first divider.
- Bubbles stay. Three product knobs: `--chat-own-bg`, `--chat-own-ink`, `--chat-bubble-edge`, read with fallbacks so each scope's accent is the one used. Defaults are what was drawn, so nothing moves.
- Avatars: `.cc-space` (a rounded square in the accent: a project with people of its own) and `.cc-plain` (no gap, no ring).
- Lists: `.list-title`, `.list-sub`, `.list-item.cc-unread`, `.dot.cc-accent`. A selected row (`aria-current` on the row) is filled as a selected link row is, and a row whose link is nested in its text now takes the row's hover, selected and focus treatment too.

## Consequences

The planning app can re-vendor and delete its `cv-*` copies. The bench's glitches are not copied: emoji draw at the control size in a color face, the hover time stays inside its column, the typing line starts at the text, the phone hint is gone on touch and one line elsewhere, the top divider cannot be clipped. Not done: a knob mapping a product's own kinds to shapes (a product with a fourth kind sets `--av-radius` and `--av-color` in context); the conversation header is a documented composition, not a class.
