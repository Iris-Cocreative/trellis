# D77. The sheet becomes a tray, on any side

## Context

`.modal.cc-sheet` opened from the bottom, edge to edge, and under 48rem every modal became the same bottom sheet; its durations were `--dur-sheet-in` and `--dur-sheet-out`. The owner found "sheet" the wrong word and asked for a tray, on the left by default with margins at the top and bottom, and options for every side.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, rename and add sides, `.modal.cc-tray` with `.cc-left` (the default), `.cc-right`, `.cc-top` and `.cc-bottom`, the attached edge flush, the two ends inset by a two-cell `--tray-margin`, the free side's corners round, a centered modal becoming a bottom tray under 48rem and a side tray staying a drawer, with `.cc-sheet` and the sheet tokens kept one minor as aliases; B, rename only, still at the bottom; C, keep the name and add sides (`.cc-sheet.cc-left`). And where the default left tray sits: C1, flush to the screen's left edge with margins top and bottom, a drawer; C2, floating, inset on all four sides.

## Decision

A and C1. `.modal.cc-tray` slides in from its side on the spring curve, flush to that edge, its two ends inset by `--tray-margin` (two cells), its free side's corners round; left by default, `.cc-right`, `.cc-top`, `.cc-bottom`. A side tray is `--tray-w` wide (32 cells), its body scrolls and its footer stays at the end. Under 48rem a centered modal becomes a bottom tray, and a side tray stays a drawer, leaving four cells of the page to tap. The durations are `--dur-tray-in` and `--dur-tray-out`.

## Consequences

Breaking, softened by aliases: `.cc-sheet` (the bottom, edge to edge, as before) and `--dur-sheet-in`/`--dur-sheet-out` stay one minor. A centered modal on a narrow screen now has two cells at each end instead of reaching the screen's edges. The modal's padding splits into `--modal-gutter-block` and `--modal-gutter-inline`, its corners into four variables, and it gains `--modal-max-h`. `tests/layout.js` checks the left tray is flush and two cells from the top and the bottom.
