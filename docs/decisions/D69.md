# D69. R16 says what core does

## Context

R16 said one `.float` serves every popover, options card and tooltip, in the top layer, following its anchor and closing when the anchor scrolls away. Core puts `.float[popover]` in the top layer at a fixed position and defines its motion once; following the anchor and closing on scroll were the specimen's own script.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, keep the rule as written, questioned until Floats that follow and close moves following and closing into Trellis; B, reword it to what core does and mark it settled.

## Decision

B. R16 now reads: one `.float` for every popover, options card and tooltip; core puts it in the top layer at a fixed position and defines its motion once; placing it by its anchor, following the anchor and closing when it scrolls away are the app's job. It still aligns to its anchor, not the grid. Settled.

## Consequences

No code changes. The action Floats that follow and close stays an idea for later, not a debt the rule owes. A4's float part says the same.
