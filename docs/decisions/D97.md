# D97. Night has its own ground, and may be a dusk

## Context

The dark theme drew every ground and edge from the same seed as the light one, at fixed lightnesses: core's night is .176 for the page. The community app, moving onto Trellis (D96), needs two things that can't be said that way. Its home space is linen by day and forest by night: another hue after dark, not a darker linen. And its studio's top bar is a dusk blue (`#34445a`, lightness .39), a dark ground lighter than any night core draws.

## Options considered

A third theme value beside light and dark. Every role is `light-dark()`, which knows two; a third would double the palette's declarations, and a dusk is still dark: light ink on a dark ground.

A ground seed per theme, and a lift for the night ladder. Two knobs, both following what is there, so nothing moves.

## Decision

- `--seed-night`: the hue and chroma of every ground and edge at night. It follows the ground, so a product that leaves it sees no change.
- `--night-lift`: added to the lightness of every ground and edge at night. 0, core's night, by default; about .15 is a dusk. Ink climbs toward white three times as fast (each text role's night lightness L becomes L + (1 − L) × min(1, 3 × lift)), so a dusk keeps every contrast floor: at .15 tertiary ink on the third raised ground is 4.55:1. Past about .16 a nested ground fails, and the contract says so.
- Both are knobs: a product sets them, and a scope may set its own (D96), so one part of a page can be dusk while the rest is night. `math.js` has both (`seeds().night`, `color.nightLift`); the contract reads them per scope; `tests/math.js` checks a night seed at lift 0 and .15 in both themes.

## Consequences

The community app's home space can be linen by day and forest by night from one product layer, and its studio's bar a dusk scope. Nothing moves for a product that sets neither.
