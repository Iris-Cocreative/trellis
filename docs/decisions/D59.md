# D59. A switch primitive

## Context

The specimen's Outset, Grid, Measure and CSS controls are independent on/off settings, but sat in one segmented group like Theme and Fit, where exactly one is on, so several raised segments read as a broken radio group. Core had pressed buttons and tags, and no switch.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: a `.switch` primitive in core; no new primitive, with binary settings as separate pressed buttons with a dot that fills (shipped in the specimen as a stopgap); or filter chips, pressed tags (which blur a tag's meaning: kind).

## Decision

A `.switch` primitive in core, with the stopgap kept only until it landed. A `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`, or a `label` around a hidden checkbox with `role="switch"`. Track 3 × 1½ cells, thumb 1 cell a quarter inside it; on, the thumb moves and the track fills with the accent. Its label centers its capitals on the track (R8). Its states are the shared layer (D58).

## Consequences

Settings screens in both apps get one switch. The specimen's bar uses it for Outset, Measure and CSS; the grid, with three states, stays a button that names its state. A choice of one among several stays a segmented control.
