# D36. The first app, then the second

## Context

Two apps will adopt Trellis.

## Options considered

Not weighed aloud at the time. Options named since: the second app first, or both at once. The likely reason for the order: the first app is a rebuild with nothing to live beside, and the second must coexist with a utility framework.

## Decision

The first app adopts Trellis first, then the second.

## Consequences

Breaking changes land before the first app pins a version. The plan's initiatives are named for the two adoptions.

## Date

2026-11-07, when the owner stated the order.

## Who

The owner.

## Superseded by

None.
