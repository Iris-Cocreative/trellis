# D4. A half-unit baseline

## Context

Text needed a rhythm to sit on.

## Options considered

Not weighed at the time. Options named since: no baseline grid, with free line heights; a full-unit baseline; a 4px baseline, as other systems use. D13 records what replaced it and why.

## Decision

Text sat on a baseline grid of half units.

## Consequences

Two lines on the page: the unit and the half-unit baseline.

## Date

2026-10-28

## Who

Not recorded. It shipped in 0.2.0.

## Superseded by

D13: the cell is the only line.
