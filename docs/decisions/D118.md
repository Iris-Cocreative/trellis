# D118. Phosphor is the default set, and a weight is a stroke on its raw drawing

## Context

The owner chose Phosphor as the default icon set on 2026-11-24, with size, stroke, weight and color as variables, and instructions for a set of one's own, raster icons and native apps.

`.icon` draws strokes: `fill: none`, a stroke of 1.5 in the drawing's own units. Phosphor publishes outlined shapes on a 256-unit square, filled, in six weights, a file per weight. Inside `.icon` they draw as the outlines of outlines. The membership app's ledger asks for filled icons (13) and two-toned ones at two and a half cells (35); its rail draws Phosphor's duotone with a class of its own, beside a stroke set of fourteen units. The planning app draws Phosphor's regular outlines with a component of its own in its documents (twelve to sixteen pixels), and another stroke set elsewhere. Both work, outside `.icon`.

What was measured, on Phosphor's repository at `2b75f3a` (1,512 icons):

- Its `raw` folder holds the drawings the outlines come from, and those are strokes: 8 units wide in thin, 12 in light, 16 in regular, 24 in bold, with round caps and joins everywhere. The npm package doesn't carry them.
- Thin and light are the regular drawing at another width, but for about a dozen icons. Drawn from the regular one by core's rules and compared with Phosphor's file at 128 pixels, 1,501 match thin within a pixel, 1,503 light and 1,506 regular, in both engines within one icon. The rest differ by Phosphor's own revisions between the two folders (four hands), or by a detail moved (the two circled arrows).
- Bold is not: 697 match. Phosphor moves shapes apart so the gaps survive the heavier line.
- Duotone is the regular drawing over a shape at a fifth of the ink. Fill is a drawing of its own, with no line.
- A filled dot's radius is 4 more than half the line in thin, light, regular and most of bold (8, 10, 12, 16), so a circle of radius 4 with the same stroke is the dot at every weight. Past 16 the engines draw that circle a little small (.7 units at 20, 1.6 at 24, a tenth of a pixel on a 14px icon).

Phosphor's license, in its own repository, is MIT.

## Options considered

### How a Phosphor icon is drawn

**A filled variant of `.icon`** for the outlined files, as the ledger asks. Any icon package works. Weight is then the file: thin, light, regular and bold of each icon in the sprite, and the variable the owner asked for can't exist.

**The raw drawings as strokes in plain `.icon`,** with `--icon-stroke: 16`. No new class. But `--icon-stroke` inherits, and is in the drawing's units, so a context that sets 1.5 for a stroke set draws Phosphor as a hair; and the outlined files still don't draw.

**A font.** Phosphor has one, with a family a weight. A request, a file of every icon, weight by family, and glyphs that center on a line box and are read out or not by luck. One app came from it.

**A variable font or a filter** (a stroke added outside an outline, to fake weight). Phosphor has no variable font, and a stroke round an outlined shape closes its gaps and thickens its corners.

**One class under which both draw** (chosen): filled unless a shape says otherwise, with a stroke width to inherit. Phosphor's outlines carry no paint and are filled; its raw shapes carry `fill="none" stroke="currentColor"` and are stroked, at the width on them or, with that taken off, at the variable.

### The variable

**The four named weights as classes or tokens.** Words for four numbers, when the numbers between work too, and 20 is the right one at fourteen pixels.

**Font weights, 100 to 700.** Familiar, and not linear in the line: it would be a table in CSS.

**The line as a share of the icon, in Phosphor's units** (chosen): 8, 12, 16, 24. It is what the drawings are in.

**The line in pixels, whatever the size.** A stroke's width in user units can't be worked out from a length without typed division or `tan(atan2())`, which core refuses (it blanked every iPhone once), and `vector-effect` isn't inherited, so it is an attribute on every shape.

**A weight that follows the size by itself.** The same arithmetic. `--icon-size` is a variable an app sets, not a class core could hang a weight on. It is a table instead: 24, 20, 16 and 12 from small to large are one line of 1.1px at a 12px cell.

### Where the icons are

**A file in the package, or a tool that makes a sprite.** A dependency to keep in step with another project, for the few icons an app uses. The instructions hold the script, sixteen lines; every icon it makes was drawn and compared.

**The whole specimen on Phosphor, or the Icons section alone.** All of it, since it is the default set. The drawings that are the specimen's own (the principles' eight, a sample brand's mark) stay strokes.

### Raster

**A mask that takes the ink** (`mask-image` from a variable). It would color a one-color raster. Such a shape should be an SVG; a `url()` in a custom property resolves differently by engine; and forced colors would erase it. Not built.

## Decision

- **`.icon.cc-phosphor`**: 256 units, filled in the ink unless a shape says otherwise, its strokes `--icon-weight` wide (16). Phosphor's outlined files, its raw files as they come, and raw drawings with their width taken off all draw under it. `.icon` alone is as it was, in every way.
- **`--icon-weight`** is a knob, and any context may set it. It reaches drawings in strokes with no width of their own. Bold, fill and duotone are other drawings.
- **`--icon-color`** is a role on an icon of either kind, or in a context. It has no weight of its own in the cascade: any color set on the icon, a disabled control and forced colors come first.
- **`.icon-tone`** is a second shape, a fifth of the ink unless `--icon-tone` says otherwise; forced colors take it back.
- **`.cc-mirror`** turns an icon that points along the line, in right-to-left text, by its own direction (`:dir()`).
- **`img.icon`** is fitted whole in its box, its corners kept, in prose too.
- **`skill/ICONS.md`**, in the package: use, weight and its limits, one line at every size, adding a Phosphor icon with its notice, drawing on Phosphor's grid or a set of one's own, variants by size, raster, and a table of sizes and weights for Material Symbols and SF Symbols.
- **The specimen** carries its 32 drawings and Phosphor's notice in its own markup.

## Consequences

Nothing that rendered moves: the specimen as it was, under this core and the last, 0 of 177,756 boxes and painted styles differ over 42 pages (core, Quarry, Almanac; wide, phone, 312%; both themes; both engines; forced colors in Chromium). The specimen's own icons changed, and are in boxes of the same size.

An app's stroke set keeps working beside Phosphor; its drawings and Phosphor's regular are different lines (1.5px and .9px at fourteen pixels), so a place holds one or the other.

A color set on an `.icon` by a class stays that color in forced colors, in Chromium, as it did (an SVG keeps its author's colors there). `--icon-color` doesn't.

Material's column in the native table is measured from its published drawings. SF Symbols' is worked out from what Apple says of its scales, since the symbols have no box and were not measured on a device.

`tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide and touch, reads each kind of drawing as an amount of ink, and the color, size, knob, mirror and forced colors; 28 mutations each fail it.

Animated icons, and Figma's set, are later.
