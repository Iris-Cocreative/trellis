# D15. Touch targets grow invisibly

## Context

Dense controls are smaller than a finger.

## Options considered

The review session proposed that controls become visibly taller on touch, four cells high. The build session grew the hit area invisibly instead, so density is unchanged.

Not weighed at the time. Options named since: stay at the 24px accessibility minimum; use 48, as Android's guidance does.

## Decision

On a coarse pointer every control's hit area is at least `--hit-min` (44px, Apple's guidance), whatever it draws: an invisible `::before` grows around it.

## Consequences

Rule R18. Density never costs a missed tap. Inputs on touch also take at least 16px type, or iOS zooms the page.

## Date

2026-11-02

## Who

The build session, in answer to a point the review session raised. It shipped in 0.5.0.
