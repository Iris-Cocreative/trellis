# D13. The cell is the only line

## Context

With a unit and a half-unit baseline, the page had two lines to keep, and text and boxes did not share one rhythm.

## Options considered

Keep the half-unit baseline (D4), or make the cell the only line. The cell was chosen.

## Decision

The half-unit baseline is gone. Headlines (h1 to h3) stand on the cells. Other text hangs from a cell line by its cap top, keeps its own line height, and rounds its block up to whole cells (`calc-size()`, or `settle.js` where Safari lacks it).

## Consequences

This is the rhythm model, the most expensive part of the API to change: a change to it is a major changelog entry. The cell's target became 10px on a phone, rising to 12px.
