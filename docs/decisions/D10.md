# D10. An even count and whole modules

## Context

A fitted screen needs its structure to be symmetric about its center, whatever its width.

## Options considered

Not weighed at the time. Options named since: any count, though an odd one puts a cell, not a line, at the center; a fixed gutter, with the remainder left over; a module of another size. The last is open again: a direction under discussion, not decided, would let the module follow a product's proportion.

## Decision

The cell count is even, so the screen's center is a line. The gutter takes 2 or 3 cells so the field is whole 4-cell modules (the gutter parity rule). On wide screens the frame steps by a module.

## Consequences

Every fourth line in from the field's edges is a strong line. `math.js` holds the parity rule and `contract.js` checks its worked examples.

## Date

2026-10-29

## Who

The owner. The 0.4.0 commit note gives the owner's model: the strongest lines come in modules of four, symmetric on the device.
