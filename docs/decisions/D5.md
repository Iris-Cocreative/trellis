# D5. Fit the cell to touch screens

## Context

On a phone the screen's edge is the frame. A whole-pixel cell leaves a remainder, so the grid cannot span the screen.

## Options considered

Fit everywhere: rejected, because on a desktop window every space would breathe while the window is dragged. Fit on 1× screens: rejected, because a fractional cell there draws equal gaps as 5px and 6px. Fit on touch screens at 1.5× and up: chosen.

## Decision

On a coarse pointer at 1.5× and up, whole cells span the screen: an even count, and the cell is the width divided by it. Desktop windows keep a whole cell by default; `data-unit-fit` forces or refuses the fit.

## Consequences

The cell becomes fractional on phones; lines never do. Gutters clear the safe areas. Chrome's device emulation cannot show the micro scale, so WebKit is the reference.
