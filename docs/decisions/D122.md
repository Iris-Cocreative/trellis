# D122. A bubble is a shaped ground, with no edge

## Context

The owner, looking at the planning app's draft: "I think we'll have to find a better way to do speech bubbles. These will read as buttons." A bubble was a raised ground, a hairline edge, a cell of padding and the card's corner, which is a secondary button's recipe; your own (D65) was the accent with its ink, which is the primary button exactly. Where a product's card and control corners are the same (the planning app's are both 6px), the speech tail, a quarter cell, was too small to see. A one-line message was three cells tall, a button two and a half.

A mockup (`experiments/bubbles/`, not committed) drew the same thread seven ways, in core and the planning app's draft layer, by day and at night, wide and in a phone's column, beside the buttons it could be taken for.

## Options considered

**A, today's.** A button, as above.

**B, on the ground:** no box for anyone, yours told apart by alignment alone. The best contrast, but a long message of yours is ragged on the left, and two short ones read as one paragraph.

**C, a shaped ground, no edge:** a faint wash of the text, a corner rounder than any control's, and on the first message of a run the top corner on the speaker's side square, under the name. Your own on a faint wash of the accent, in the text's ink.

**D, only your own tinted;** **E, flat rows** (core's `.cc-flat`, as it is); **F, one ground per run**, the washes of a run meeting; **G, D with C's shape**, the mockup's recommendation.

## Decision

C, as core's default. The owner chose it on 2026-11-28.

- **Everyone's bubble is a ground, not a fill.** Others': `--chat-bubble-wash` (7%) of the text mixed into the surface in OKLab. Yours: `--chat-own-wash` (20%) of the accent, in the text's ink. No edge. What tells yours apart is its place at the row's end, no avatar, its meta after it, and the accent in its ground; never a solid accent, the button's cue.
- **The shape is speech's own.** `--chat-shape` (18px, a cell and a half) is the bubble's corner, apart from `--shape-card`, so a product with square cards keeps round speech; a dense context takes the smaller of it and a cell. The first message of a run squares its top corner on the speaker's side (`--chat-head`, 0): others' at the start, yours at the end, so in right-to-left writing they swap. A continuing message (`.cc-cont`) is round all round. The bottom corners are round: the tail (`--chat-tail`, D65) follows the corner now, and a context can still tighten it.
- **Knobs.** A product sets `--chat-shape`, `--chat-bubble-pad` (a cell), `--chat-bubble-wash` and `--chat-own-wash`; and the three D99 gave, kept: `--chat-bubble-bg` and `--chat-own-bg` are laid over their wash (transparent by default; an opaque color replaces it, a translucent one tints it), `--chat-own-ink` is your words' ink (the text), and `--chat-bubble-edge` draws an edge (none). A product that set `--chat-own-bg: var(--accent)` and `--chat-own-ink: var(--accent-ink)` keeps its solid bubble.
- **Contrast is held where it is set.** On a bubble's ground the third ink reads as the second (the "(edited)" mark, a link's site), as on a chosen card (D116). `tools/product.js` holds the text and the second ink on others' ground and your own, your own words in `--chat-own-ink`, over every surface level in both themes, for the root and every scope, with the layer's own washes and colors; a wash that is not a plain percentage, or a color it can't read (anything but transparent, a role, a color, or `color-mix(in oklab, …)` of a role and a role, the surface or transparent), is refused. `tests/contract.js` holds it for core, every layer and scope and 48 accents, and checks that core draws what the product check computes.
- **The quote's two lines.** In a narrow column `.chat-quote-text` showed the top of its third line under the ellipsis, in both engines: its box was held open by padding given back as margin, and a role rounds its box up to whole cells. Its clip is measured from the top now, as `.u-clamp`'s is (D105): where a clamp's box is trimmed (with `calc-size()`, today), two lines from the capitals' top and a descender's depth; where it is not (Safari), two whole lines from the first line's top.

## Consequences

Every bubble looks different: no edge, a rounder corner with one square, a fainter ground, and your own no longer a solid accent. No box moves: the padding is the cell it was. On the specimen, in both engines, at seven screens, layers and themes, nothing outside the chat block changed its box or its paint.

A product that copied the template's old line, `--chat-own-bg: var(--accent)`, without `--chat-own-ink`, now has the text's ink on the accent; `tools/product.js` fails it, and the fix is `--chat-own-ink: var(--accent-ink)`, or dropping both.

The Figma file draws the bubble of D65 and D99 until its thread is generated again; `docs/figma/README.md` says what it needs.

`tests/layout.js`, both engines, wide and touch: the corners of the first and a continuing message, others' and yours, left to right and right to left, dense, and with `--chat-shape` at 0; no edge and a cell of padding; a faint, opaque ground under which the words, the second ink and the third read at 4.5:1 as drawn, in both themes, on the page, a card and a card in a card; none on a wall, a system line, a flat row or a removed message, which keeps its dashed edge and its third ink; each knob, the old three included, reaches the bubble. From a screenshot, a two-line quote in a narrow column is two bands of ink of one depth and nothing under them. Each fails without its rule.

Not decided here: tagging a version; the dense chat corner past taking the smaller of the two.
