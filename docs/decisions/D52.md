# D52. Controls grow past the cap

## Context

Above the cell's cap (D46), control labels follow the reader (they are in rem) while control heights are in cells, which stop. At 312% text, buttons, tags and segmented controls overflowed their rows and pushed the page sideways.

## Options considered

Heights follow the label, in whole cells; control type stops at the cap like the cell; or leave controls overflowing and test text only. Heights follow the label.

## Decision

A control is never shorter than its own type. Past the cap a control grows by whole cells from its own height. At default sizes every label is shorter than its control, so nothing moves.

## Consequences

Rule R17 gains the clause. Button, icon button, tag, input, tab and badge. Carried by Controls past the cap.
