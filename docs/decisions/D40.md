# D40. What stays private

## Context

Two things were written about private codebases: an inventory that audits them, and the product layers.

## Options considered

Publishing the two in-house product layers as examples was raised as the owner's call and declined, in favor of a neutral specimen and invented example layers. Publishing the inventory or a client's layer was never on the table.

## Decision

The inventory and the product layers stay private and live with their products. The public specimen shows only core's neutral defaults. Two invented example layers keep the knob mechanism tested.

## Consequences

The specimen and the tests take one outside layer by address, so a private layer runs through the same tests from where it lives. Keeping private files off every public site is its own item.
