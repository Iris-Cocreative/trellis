# D19. Clusters round to the nearest cell

## Context

A wrapping row of tags or chips has its own half-cell rhythm inside.

## Options considered

In 0.5.0 a cluster rounded up, like text. A row of one-and-a-half-cell tags took two cells and left more space after it. 0.6.0 changed it to the nearest cell, halves down, so a dense row takes less space after it, not more.

## Decision

A cluster rounds its height to the nearest cell, halves down. Its content may overflow into the gap after it by up to half a cell.

## Consequences

Safe only because the gap after a cluster is whole cells and at least one (R6). `math.js` has `settleCluster()`. D55 adds a clause: at the end of a surface, where only its padding follows, a cluster rounds up.
