# D19. Clusters round to the nearest cell

## Context

A wrapping row of tags or chips has its own half-cell rhythm inside.

## Options considered

Not recorded. The sources say what was chosen, not what else was weighed. Flagged for the owner.

## Decision

A cluster rounds its height to the nearest cell, halves down. Its content may overflow into the gap after it by up to half a cell.

## Consequences

Safe only because the gap after a cluster is whole cells and at least one (R6). `math.js` has `settleCluster()`.
