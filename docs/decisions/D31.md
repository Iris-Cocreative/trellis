# D31. Components and lists are another layer

## Context

Apps render components and content lists from data.

## Options considered

The owner asked whether Trellis should include its own component and content-list mechanism. Chosen instead: Trellis owns the contract, and the renderer owns the machinery.

## Decision

Trellis owns the contract for a component or a collection. Props, data binding and editing belong to the renderer.

## Consequences

The Component and collection contract is a kit item. `REACT.md` is the contract for one renderer.

## Date

2026-11-10.

## Who

The owner.
