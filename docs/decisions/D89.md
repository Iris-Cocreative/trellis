# D89. A heading is never smaller than body text

## Context

Headings step down from H1 by the ratio, five times, whatever the ratio is. Body is its own anchor. So how many headings fit above body depends on how far H1 is from it: at the defaults H1 is 4.41 steps of 1.333 above body in a wide space, and the fifth step, h6, came out at 15.2 where body is 18 (14.5 against 16 in a narrow space). A wider ratio runs out sooner. At root two H1 is 3.66 steps above body, so h5 and h6 fell under it; at the golden ratio 2.64, so h4 did too, and h6 came out at 5.8.

The specimen's ratio playground showed it: a heading under body text was drawn fainter and named in a sentence. It could say so and could not prevent it, since the sizes were core's.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time:

Leaving the sizes and flagging them, as the playground did. A product that chose a wide ratio then shipped headings smaller than the text they head, and an editor's h6 was smaller than its paragraph in every product.

A floor at body's size.

Named while building it, not weighed at the time:

A floor one text step above body, so a held heading is still larger. It makes h5 and h6 the size of `.t-lg`, and a heading's size then depends on the text step as well as the ratio.

A knob for the floor. Nothing has asked for a heading under body text, and a knob nobody turns is one more number to carry.

Fitting the headings between H1 and body, so the step is whatever divides the distance by five. The ratio is then no longer the one the product chose.

## Decision

Each of a heading's two ends is floored at body's: its narrow end at body's narrow end, its wide end at body's wide end. Between them it runs in a straight line, as every role does, so it is never under body text at any width.

A heading held at the floor is body's size in the heading's family and weight, with its own line and its own space after it. Two headings held there are the same size.

H1 is not capped. A product that wants more headings apart at a wide ratio raises H1.

## Consequences

- At core's defaults h6 goes from 14.5 to 16 in a narrow space and from 15.2 to 18 in a wide one. Nothing else moves.
- In the examples: Quarry is unchanged; Almanac's h5 and h6 are held at body's size at both ends (17 and 20), where they were 13.9 and 10.5 narrow, 17.4 and 11.6 wide.
- `core.css` writes each heading's `--t-min` and `--t-max` as `max()` of body's and its own. `math.js`: `typeEnds()` applies the floor, and `typeSteps()` gives the ends before it, for showing the working.
- The specimen's math view says when an end is held, and the playground's flag for a heading under body text now marks one held at body's size.
- `tests/math.js` reads every heading's size from the browser, on the page and in the playground's two boxes, and fails one smaller than body's.
- There is no knob: a product cannot put a heading under body text.
