# D70. Time gets a ratio; the easings are in, out and spring

## Context

F2 said durations come from one small set and three easings, named enter, exit and move. The six durations were 50ms steps plus the skeleton, not a ratio from a seed as space and type are, and the easing tokens were named in, out and spring.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: For the durations: A, a ratio from a seed, like type, built by the action Motion proportions; B, reword F2 to one small set of tokens and keep the steps. For the names: 1, the README's easings become in, out and spring; 2, the tokens are renamed enter, exit and move (breaking).

## Decision

A1. Durations will be a ratio from a seed, built by Motion proportions, so F2 stays questioned and says so. The README names the easings as the tokens do: in, out, spring.

## Consequences

No code changes now. F2 is questioned until Motion proportions lands; its page says that is the open work. The token names stand.
