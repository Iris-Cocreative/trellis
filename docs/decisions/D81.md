# D81. Depth is the ground

## Context

A card inside a card painted the same ground and was told apart only by its edge. Floats and modals carried a shadow. A nested card's corner was its own, not concentric with its parent's. Rows in a list divided by hairlines from the row's text inset, under a leading avatar too. A tray had no grabber, and nothing happened to what was behind it when another opened. The plan's condition for stacked surfaces named the rules: depth from the ground, concentric corners, grouped rows with hairlines from the text, a grabber and a peek by whole cells, three levels at most, only the top taking input.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan:

1. The ground at each level: A, a three-step ladder read from nesting; B, alternate wells and raised surfaces; C, one ground, depth from the edge only.
2. Shadow: A, cards take none, floats and modals keep `--lift`; B, no shadows anywhere.
3. Corners: concentric, as `.card-media` already was.
4. Grouped rows: A, `.list.cc-group`, one surface, rules from the text; B, rows in a `.card` with only the hairline changed.
5. Trays over trays: A, a grabber, the one behind stepping back to peek by two cells, three at most; B, the grabber only.

## Decision

1A, 2A, 3, 4A, 5A. The owner added to 2A that "cards never take a shadow" is a default, not a hard rule.

- Two roles join the neutral ladder: `--bg-raised-2` (light 0.985, dark 0.245) and `--bg-raised-3` (0.97, 0.277). A card or grouped list inside a card, a grouped list, a modal or a float takes the second; one more level in, the third; deeper stays at the third. `--surface-level` says which.
- A nested card's corner is its parent's less the parent's smaller padding, never under a quarter cell. Each level reads the level above from a variable that level alone writes, so no declaration refers to itself.
- `.list.cc-group`: a card's ground, edge and corner on the list, which steps out as a card does. Each rule is an outset border image on the row's body, clipped sideways by the row, so it starts at the text whatever the leading mark is and adds no size.
- `.modal-grabber` draws on a bottom tray (and a centered modal that becomes one on a narrow screen). `settle.js` keeps the order of open modals: `--modal-index` on each, `data-trellis-depth` (1, 2) on those under the top one; it closes the third when a fourth opens. A centered modal or bottom tray under another steps up two cells per level.

The card said the tray behind "steps back and its top peeks by two cells". Shrinking the tray in front by two cells was tried first; it only shows the one behind when both are as tall as the screen allows, so the one behind steps up instead, which shows it at any height.

## Consequences

- Every nested card in an app changes ground and corner. No app ships on Trellis yet.
- The contract holds ink at 4.5:1 on both new grounds. At the default seeds tertiary ink is 4.56:1 on level 3 in light and 4.65:1 in dark: the narrowest margins on the ladder.
- A side tray under another stays put.
- A modal is a surface for counting levels: a card in a modal is level 2.
- New rule R21.
