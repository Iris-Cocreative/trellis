# D78. The specimen shows icons as they are, and color as it is made

## Context

The owner asked for a section on color (defaults, colors set in relation to others, gradients from base colors) and one on icons. Core has `.icon`, a stroke sized in cells, centered on the cap height or the x-height (R9), with no chosen set, no delivery format and no stroke rule. The palette had just become relational (D54), with nowhere in the specimen to see it.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan, for icons: A, a specimen section on today's `.icon` only, its sizes and rule 9's centering; B, A plus choosing a set, its delivery (a sprite or inline) and its stroke (1px lines that never scale, or the set's own 2px), which fixes a public rule and takes on a dependency.

## Decision

A, specimen only. The Icons section shows the four sizes, rule 9's two centerings, an overhang as the component's choice (R12 is withdrawn, D68) and the stroke knob. It ships with the Color section approved with the palette: the seeds, every role as a light and dark pair side by side with its contrast measured in the browser, the mixes a role makes into a ground, and the two gradient tokens. The four seed cards draw past their own edges.

## Consequences

No framework change. B stays the Icons action's own decision, where a 1px practice and a set's 2px default can be weighed.
