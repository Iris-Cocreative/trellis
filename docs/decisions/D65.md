# D65. Your own message, and runs

## Context

`.cc-own` only reversed the row and tinted the bubble, so your own avatar sat at the far right, a lone time floated above the bubble, and a one-line bubble with the same corner on every side became a pill. The first app's messaging reads better: sent bubbles in a solid send color with white ink, the speaker's bottom corner tightened, the meta under the bubble at its end.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on a decision card in the plan: A, the first app's sent-bubble pattern in variables: your avatar hidden, a solid accent bubble in the accent's ink, a tight end-bottom corner, the meta after the bubble, and others keep the avatar with their start-bottom corner tightened the same way; B, quiet own: the tint kept, the avatar hidden, the meta after, the tail tightened; C, A or B plus consecutive messages from one speaker sharing one avatar and one meta.

## Decision

A plus C. Your own message is a solid `--accent` bubble in `--accent-ink` at the row's end, with no avatar and its meta after the bubble; every bubble tightens its speaker's bottom corner to `--chat-tail` (a quarter cell). `.cc-cont` marks a message that continues the one before it from the same speaker: its avatar keeps its place unseen, the meta shows once (above the first of others' runs, under the last of your own), and the run is a cell apart whatever the stack's gap.

## Consequences

Variables only (R13): `--chat-ink`, `--chat-tail`, `--chat-avatar`, `--chat-meta-order`, and `.cc-cont`. The accent's ink is computed per fill (D54), so the solid bubble reads in both themes. The last message of your own run is found with `:has()`, which every supported browser has.
