# D94. Root two is the default wide ratio, and H1 is 72

## Context

Core's headings stepped down by a perfect fourth, 1.333, in a wide space, from an H1 of 64. The owner wanted to try root two, 1.414, where two steps double a size, and said the default heading was smaller than it had to be.

A wider step runs out of room sooner: H1 at 64 is 3.66 steps of root two above body's 18, so h5 and h6 would both be held at body's size (D89). A trial page set today's ratio beside root two with H1 at 64 and at 72, on the real roles, and two trial layers put the whole specimen under each.

## Options considered

Weighed at the time, on the trial page:

Staying at 1.333 and 64.

Root two with H1 at 64. Two headings held at body's size in a wide space.

Root two with H1 at 72. H1 is four steps above body, so h5 lands on body's size by the ratio and only h6 is held.

Named while building it, not weighed at the time: the narrow ratio. The trial kept 1.2. Under D93 the narrow ratio also sets body and H1 in a narrow space, and the playground's own pairing for root two is 1.26, where three steps double a size: body comes out at 16.04 and H1 at 40.4. At 1.2 body would be 16.4 and H1 34.

## Decision

The default ratio is 1.26 in a narrow space opening to 1.414 in a wide one, and H1 in a wide space is 72.

## Consequences

- Breaking: every default size but body's and the label's wide end moves. In points, narrow to wide: H1 40.4 to 72 (was 36 to 64), h2 32.1 to 50.9, h3 25.5 to 36, h4 20.2 to 25.5, h5 16 to 18, h6 16 to 18 (held), xl 20.2 to 25.5, lg 18 to 21.4, body 16 to 18, sm 14.3 to 15.1, xs 12.7 at both ends, the eyebrow 13.5 to 14.3, the label 12.
- xs is the same size at both ends, so the README's known wrinkle no longer applies to core's defaults.
- The trial page and its two layers are gone.
