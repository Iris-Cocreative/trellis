# D88. A surface on a surface is drawn by its ground alone

## Context

D81 gave nested surfaces a step of the neutral ladder each, three at most. On the specimen the three levels read as three outlined boxes: every level kept its hairline edge, the light ladder's steps were 0.015 of lightness apart (1, 0.985, 0.97), each level padded two cells so every level's text started a cell further in, and the inner corners came out nearly square, because a corner was its parent's less the parent's padding (18 less 24) when the real gap between the two edges was one cell. The owner asked what would make it feel designed.

## Options considered

Leaving it, and dressing the specimen's example. The example was honest: nested cards looked like that in any product.

Shadows for depth. Rule 21 is that depth never depends on a shadow.

Keeping the edges and only fixing the corners. An outline inside an outline inside an outline is a wireframe whatever its corners do.

Ground alone, with the steps made visible. The steps could not open while tertiary ink sat at its floor: at 0.544 it reads at 4.56:1 on 0.97, so the third level could not go darker.

## Decision

A surface on a surface is drawn by its ground alone.

- **No edge.** A card inside a card, a grouped list, a modal or a float sets `--card-border` to transparent. The first level keeps its edge: the page and a card are too close in tone to part without one. In forced colors, where the ground is gone, the edge comes back.
- **The ladder opens in light:** `--bg-raised-2` 0.975 and `--bg-raised-3` 0.95, steps of 0.025. `--text-tertiary` goes to 0.52 in light, so it reads at 4.76:1 on the third level and better everywhere else. Dark is unchanged.
- **One cell of padding.** With the step out of one cell, a nested card's text stands on the line its parent's text stands on.
- **A cell between the edges, always.** A nested card steps out only into padding its parent can spare: a cell in a two-cell card, nothing in a one-cell card. Where it opens or closes its parent it steps out at that end too, so the same cell parts the edges all round.
- **Corners concentric with the real gap:** the parent's corner less the gap between the two edges, never under a quarter cell. At the defaults: 18, 6, 3.

## Consequences

- Breaking for nested cards: no edge, tighter padding, a different corner, and in a dense card no step out. `--card-border` and `--card-pad` on the nested card put the edge and the padding back.
- Tertiary ink is a little darker in light, on every surface.
- A third-level card does not step out when its parent pads one cell, so its text is a cell in from the two levels above it.
- `tests/layout.js` checks the gap, the corner against the gap, the padding and the missing edge; `tests/contract.js` still holds every ink at its floor on all three levels.
