# R18 · Touch targets

## Statement

On a coarse pointer every control's hit area is at least `--hit-min` (44px, Apple's guidance), whatever it draws: an invisible `::before` grows around it, so density never costs a missed tap.

## Why

Density is a design choice; the size of a fingertip isn't. Growing the hit area instead of the drawn control keeps dense layouts dense.

## The failure it prevents

A 24px tag that is easy to see and hard to tap.

## How it is checked

`tests/layout.js` on 393×852 touch checks that inputs are at least 44px with 16px type, and that the shell's tab bar links are at least 44px. Buttons and tags grow through `::before`, which no test measures.

## Example

A 2-cell button on a phone draws 20px tall and takes taps across 44px.

## Exceptions

A replaced element such as an input has no `::before`, so the box itself reaches the target.
