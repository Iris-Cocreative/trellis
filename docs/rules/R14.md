# R14 · Products turn knobs

## Statement

A product layer only sets variables on `:root`. Primitives read the knobs; variants and contexts set the primitive's own variables, so a product never overrides a variant by accident.

## Why

If a product could restyle a primitive, every product would become its own fork of the foundation. Knobs keep one seed that every product reshapes the same way.

## The failure it prevents

A product's `.card { padding: … }` that beats `.cc-dense` and breaks density in that product only.

## How it is checked

`tests/contract.js`: a product layer must be one `:root[data-product]` rule in `@layer trellis.product` that sets nothing but variables; every knob it sets must be in `product.template.css` with core's default. It checks both example layers and any layer given as a path.

## Example

`examples/almanac.css` sets fonts, ratios, a palette, shapes and density, and nothing else.

## Exceptions

None.
