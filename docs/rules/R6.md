# R6 · Between blocks, space is whole cells

## Statement

Between stacked blocks, space is whole cells. `.u-gap-q` and `.u-gap-h` reach clusters only, never a stack. A cluster rounds its height to the nearest cell, halves down, so its content can overflow into the gap after it by up to half a cell; that is safe only because that gap is whole cells and at least one. At the end of a surface (the last cluster in a card, a modal body or a member tile) nothing follows but the surface's padding, so there it rounds up instead. (D55) A row that holds half-cell padding is one thing, like a button: its edges stay on lines and its contents keep their own rhythm (`.list.cc-dense`).

## Why

A block's position is the sum of every block above it. A half-cell gap anywhere puts everything after it off the grid.

## The failure it prevents

A page that is on the cells down to one half-cell gap, and half a cell off for the rest of its length.

## How it is checked

`tests/align.js` measures the top of every text block and box down the whole specimen, so one half-cell gap shows as everything after it off. `tests/perf.js` checks 5,000 blocks stay on their lines after a first settle, a changed row and 1,000 added rows.

## Example

A `.stack` with a 2-cell gap between a heading and a card. A `.cluster` of tags with a half-cell gap between the tags, inside it.

## Exceptions

Inside a cluster or a control, things keep their own half-cell rhythm, like letters in a word. The 0.7.0 change made the gap utilities reach clusters only.
