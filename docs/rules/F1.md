# F1 · Rest on the lattice, move off it

## Statement

Layout places things on cells. Every motion starts and ends at a rest position on the lattice.

## Why

In between, motion can go anywhere, because nothing is measured mid-flight. At rest, a page has to be on the cells for everything else to be.

## The failure it prevents

An animation that ends a few pixels from where it started, leaving a card off the grid.

## How it is checked

Review only. The tests measure rest states only, after the runtime has settled.

## Example

A tray slides in from beyond the screen's edge and comes to rest with its ends on cell lines.

## Exceptions

None. In 0.9.0 the clause "motion is transform and opacity only" was withdrawn; what motion may use is still to be written.
