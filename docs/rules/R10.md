# R10 · Marks never stretch a line

## Statement

Marks inside text never stretch a line: code, mono, bold, italic, links and the other inline marks get `line-height: 0` inside running text. Plain spans are left alone. In `.prose`, sub and sup are moved rather than baseline-shifted.

## Why

A mark with a different font or size can carry a taller line box. One such mark in a paragraph makes that line taller than its neighbors, and the block's height stops being what its line count says.

## The failure it prevents

A paragraph with one line of inline code that is a pixel or two taller than the rest, pushing the block, and everything after it, off its count.

## How it is checked

Review only for the rule itself. `tests/align.js` catches the effect: a stretched line moves the blocks after it off their lines, and the sample post in `.prose` carries code, links, bold, italic, sub and sup.

## Example

`<p>Set <code>--outset</code> to 0.</p>`: the code's line box contributes no height, so the paragraph is its body line height.

## Exceptions

Plain `span`s are not marks and keep their line height, so a component built from spans isn't flattened by accident.
