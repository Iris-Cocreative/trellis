# R21 · Depth is the ground

## Statement

A surface on a surface takes the next step of the neutral ladder, never a shadow by default. A card, a grouped list, a modal or a float is level 1 (`--bg-raised`); a card or grouped list inside one is level 2 (`--bg-raised-2`); inside that, level 3 (`--bg-raised-3`), where it stops. Nested corners are concentric. Rows in a group share one surface and divide by hairlines that start at the text. A bottom tray keeps a grabber, a modal or bottom tray under another peeks by whole cells, three are open at most, and only the top one takes input.

## Why

A shadow says "above" by blurring an edge, and a stack of shadows turns muddy and costs paint. A ground step says the same with a color the palette already derives, keeps every edge on its line, and holds in both themes: in light a nested surface steps back toward the page, so it reads as set in; in dark each step is lighter, as a dark interface shows height. Concentric corners keep the gap between two edges even all the way round. Three levels is as deep as a reader follows without losing which surface they are on.

## The failure it prevents

A card in a card that is only told apart by a second hairline; corners that pinch where a nested card's radius equals its parent's; a stack of modals whose order cannot be seen; a fourth sheet over three.

## How it is checked

`tests/layout.js`: the specimen's three nested cards take `--bg-raised`, `--bg-raised-2` and `--bg-raised-3` and say levels 1 to 3; each nested corner is its parent's less the parent's padding; grouped rows are whole cells and every row but the last draws its rule; three bottom trays open with depths 2, 1 and none, the one behind two cells above the one in front, a grabber on each, and a fourth closes the third. Both engines, wide and touch. `tests/contract.js` holds text, secondary and tertiary ink at 4.5:1 on both new grounds, in both themes, for core and every product layer.

## Example

A settings screen: a card for an account, inside it a grouped list of devices (level 2), one of them opened in a bottom tray, and a confirmation in a second tray over it, the first peeking two cells above.

## Exceptions

Floats and modals keep `--lift`: they sit over the page, not in it. A card may take a shadow where a design asks for one; depth never depends on it. A side tray under another does not move.
