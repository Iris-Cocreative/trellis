# F3 · Free things live in layers

## Statement

An organic or animated element sits in its own layer (`.cells`, a canvas, a positioned layer), its coordinates in cells; the flow around it never depends on it.

## Why

The flow is what the rhythm model measures. Something that moves or grows freely inside the flow would move everything after it.

## The failure it prevents

A growing illustration that pushes the next section down a few pixels on every frame.

## How it is checked

Review only.

## Example

`.cells` places its children by `--cell-x` and `--cell-y` on the lattice; the stack around it sees one block of whole cells.

## Exceptions

None.
