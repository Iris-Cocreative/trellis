# F5 · Content in the field, color to the edge

## Statement

Text and controls stay in the field. A band's surface, a photograph or a rail may reach the screen's edges.

## Why

The field is where the cells are whole and the gutters protect reading. Color and images can bleed without anything becoming harder to read or reach.

## The failure it prevents

Text that runs to the screen's edge on a phone, or a band that adds sideways scroll to reach it.

## How it is checked

`tests/layout.js` checks that a cover's media reaches both screen edges and adds no sideways scroll. `tests/align.js` fails any sideways scroll on the page. `.cc-band` paints its surface with an outset border image, so it never adds scroll.

## Example

A `.cover`: the photograph runs edge to edge; its heading stays in the field.

## Exceptions

None.
