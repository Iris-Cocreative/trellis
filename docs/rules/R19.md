# R19 · The reader's text size wins; the cell stops at 1.5×

## Statement

Type follows the reader's text size all the way. The cell follows it up to 1.5 times the default (`text-scale-max`), and above that layouts reflow instead of the cells growing.

## Why

iOS and Android accessibility sizes reach about three times normal. A cell that followed all the way would turn a 40-cell phone into a 13-cell one, too few to lay anything out. Stopping the cell keeps the layout while the words keep growing, and breakpoints in rem make the layout reflow.

## The failure it prevents

A phone at the largest text size showing one word per line in a column of giant cells, or a page that scrolls sideways.

## How it is checked

`tests/align.js` with `ax` loads the specimen at 312% (the largest iOS size) in four matrix runs, two per engine, across all three layers: no sideways scroll, nothing off its line. `tests/math.js` checks `math.js` against the stylesheet; `tests/contract.js` checks that `math.js` carries `text-scale-max` from `tokens.json`.

## Example

At 312% text a 393px phone has 26 cells of 15.09px instead of about 13 cells of 30px.

## Exceptions

The dial (`--u-scale`) scales the cell after the cap; it is a separate setting.
