# R17 · Control type is fixed, and listed

## Statement

Controls sit in rows of a fixed height, so their labels don't scale with the container: `--fs-control-xs` to `--fs-control-lg` are the only font sizes outside the type roles. `--white` and `--black` are the only raw neutrals (ink on a solid color or a photo). A control is never shorter than its own type: past the cell's cap (R19) a label can outgrow its control, and then the control grows by whole cells from its own height (button, icon button, tag, input, tab, badge). At default sizes every label is shorter than its control, so nothing moves.

## Why

A control's label sized from its container would make the same button different sizes in a sidebar and a page, and a row of controls would stop lining up. A short list keeps every product's controls on one scale. "Fixed" means fixed against the container, not against the reader: the reader's text size still wins.

## The failure it prevents

A one-off 13.5px label; at the largest text sizes, labels bigger than the buttons that hold them.

## How it is checked

Review only for the list: the intended "no raw values" lint does not exist yet. The growing clause is checked indirectly by the four `tests/align.js` runs with `ax` (312% text), which fail on sideways scroll and on rows pushed off their lines. No test compares a control's height with its label directly.

## Example

A small button is 2 cells tall with a 12px label. At 312% text its label is 37px and it draws 3 cells tall.

## Exceptions

Inputs on touch take at least 16px type, or iOS zooms the page.
