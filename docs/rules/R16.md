# R16 · Floating surfaces align to their anchor

## Statement

Floating surfaces align to their anchor, not the page grid. One `.float` for every popover, options card and tooltip: in the top layer, it follows its anchor and closes when the anchor scrolls away.

## Why

A float belongs to what opened it. On the grid it would drift from its anchor whenever the anchor isn't on a cell boundary, which inside a scroller is most of the time.

## The failure it prevents

A menu that stays put while the list it came from scrolls away beneath it.

## How it is checked

Not checked by a test.

## Example

The specimen's roles menu opens under its button and closes when the scroller carries the button out of view.

## Exceptions

None.

## Questioned

The code keeps half of it. `core.css` puts `.float[popover]` in the top layer at a fixed position and defines its motion once. Following the anchor and closing when it scrolls away are done by the specimen's own script, not by Trellis, so a consumer gets neither for free. Open as "Floats that follow and close".
