# R8 · Beside a control, text centers its capitals on the control

## Statement

Text beside a control centers its capitals on the control (`.cap-center`). Both are trimmed to cap height, so centering the boxes centers the capitals.

## Why

A label and the control it names read as one line when their capitals share a center. Without trimming, the text box carries extra space above and below the capitals, and centering the boxes leaves the capitals visibly low or high.

## The failure it prevents

A row label that looks lower than the button or tag beside it.

## How it is checked

Not checked. `tests/align.js` skips `.cap-center` elements, since they are centered on a control rather than placed on a cell line.

## Example

In the specimen's tag matrix, "Kind" beside a row of tags: its capitals' middle is the tags' middle.

## Exceptions

When a matrix row stacks in a narrow container, the label above its tags is ordinary text again and stands on a line.

## Questioned

The rule assumes the text is one line. At 312% text, a row label in the specimen's tag matrix wrapped to two lines, 3.35 cells tall, and everything after it went off the lines, because cap-centered text is not rounded to whole cells. The specimen now stacks those rows by a rem breakpoint, so its test passes, but the rule has no answer for a label that wraps, and no test would catch one. Open as the idea "Cap-centered text that wraps".
