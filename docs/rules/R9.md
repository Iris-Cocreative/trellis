# R9 · An icon centers on the cap height or the x-height

## Statement

An icon beside capitals or numbers centers on the cap height. Beside lowercase text it centers on the x-height (`.snap-x`).

## Why

An icon reads as aligned when its center meets the center of the letters' visible mass. For capitals and numbers that is the cap height; for lowercase text it is the x-height.

## The failure it prevents

Icons that sit high beside lowercase text, because they were centered on capitals that aren't there.

## How it is checked

Review only. `.snap-x` trims a box to the x-height and the baseline (`text-box: trim-both ex alphabetic`); no test measures an icon against it.

## Example

A check icon beside "Saved" centers on the cap height; beside "saved changes", on the x-height.

## Exceptions

Firefox does not trim text boxes yet, so there the text sits a few pixels off and the icon with it.
